## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams want automation that doesn’t just chat—it **researches, decides, and acts**. This lesson locks the skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

ReAct: reason and act in interleaved steps—good for unknown paths.

### Beat 2

Plan-then-execute: draft a plan, optionally get approval, then run—good for auditable work.

### Beat 3

Hybrid: short plan, execute with replan on tool failure.
