Lesson 4.4 — Designing review UIs

Module: Human-in-the-loop & approvals Duration: ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive Interactive: l04-interactive.html

Learning objective

Specify a review card: summary, diff, risk, approve/edit/reject.

Teaching

The Monday-morning problem

At Acme Ops, teams want automation that doesn’t just chat—it researches, decides, and acts. This lesson locks the skill you’ll reuse all week.

Beat 1

Show proposed action, rationale, tool results, and blast radius.

Beat 2

One-click approve, edit-in-place, or reject with reason.

Beat 3

Mobile-friendly for on-call; classroom-safe demo data only.

Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

(Beat 1) “Show proposed action, rationale, tool results, and blast radius.…”

(Beat 2) “One-click approve, edit-in-place, or reject with reason.…”

(Beat 3) “You’re done with this lesson when you can apply this on an Acme Ops scenario without guessing.”
