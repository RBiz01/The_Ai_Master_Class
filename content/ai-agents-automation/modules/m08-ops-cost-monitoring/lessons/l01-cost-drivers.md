## Learning objective

Break down $/run into tokens, tool calls, retries, and human time.

## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams want automation that doesn’t just chat—it **researches, decides, and acts**. This lesson locks the skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Long contexts and tool thrash dominate spend.

### Beat 2

Retries without jitter amplify outages into bills.

### Beat 3

Track cost per successful outcome, not per raw run.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)
