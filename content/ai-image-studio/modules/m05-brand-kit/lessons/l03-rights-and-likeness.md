## Teaching

### No real faces. No surprise licenses.

Northstar / The Ai Master Class classroom rules for stills:

- **No real-person likenesses** (celebrities, influencers, coworkers)  
- **No NSFW**  
- Prefer fictional characters + owned/licensed product photography refs  
- Track tool license terms for commercial use (model-agnostic reminder: read your vendor’s terms)  
- Disclose AI assistance when client/policy requires  

```text
RIGHTS CHECK
[ ] No real likeness intended or accidental
[ ] Refs owned/licensed
[ ] Tool ToS allows this commercial use
[ ] Disclosure note prepared if required
[ ] Contact for delivery: learner@example.com (fictional training)
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If someone could name the actor, you’ve failed the brief.”

*(Beat 2)* “Rights are part of craft—not a legal afterthought.”

### Worked micro-example

Reject a “looks like famous chef” still; regenerate from fictional Nova sheet.
