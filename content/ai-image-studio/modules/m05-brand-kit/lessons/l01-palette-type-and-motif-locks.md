## Teaching

### Three locks Brand can audit

Northstar stills brand kit (excerpt):

```text
BRAND KIT — stills
Palette: #f59e0b amber · #ec4899 magenta · #0b0f19 charcoal · #e2e8f0 text
Type mood: geometric sans, confident, ample tracking on headlines
Motif: soft gradient orb, ceramic/matte materials, clean neg space
Do-not: neon clash · comic fonts · busy confetti · real likeness
```

Type may be added in layout tools; stills should leave space and not invent off-brand lettering.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If the model invents a circus font on the bottle, that’s a brand break.”

*(Beat 2)* “Motifs are memorable when they’re few—orb + ceramic, not twenty stickers.”

### Worked micro-example

Lock pasted into every prompt; layout adds DM Sans headline in neg space.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Lock palette, type mood, and a small motif set—and forbid off-brand lettering clutter.
