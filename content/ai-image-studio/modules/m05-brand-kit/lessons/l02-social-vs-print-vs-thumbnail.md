## Teaching

### One master, three jobs

Don’t regenerate three unrelated images. Adapt:

- **Social:** subject + neg space for caption/type  
- **Thumbnail:** larger subject, higher contrast, readable at tiny size  
- **Print:** resolution, bleed, soft-proof, muted compression  

```text
From master:
Social crop → protect neg space
Thumb crop → punch subject, simplify background
Print → high-res, bleed, grade soft-proof
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Thumbnails fail quietly—zoom out and squint-test.”

*(Beat 2)* “Print forgive nothing on logo edges.”

### Worked micro-example

Same bottle master → 4:5 social with left type field → 16:9 thumb with larger bottle → print with bleed.
