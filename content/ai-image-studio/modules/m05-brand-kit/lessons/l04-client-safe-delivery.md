4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Package stills for client delivery with naming, formats, rights note, and revision boundaries.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Deliver a pack, not a chat dump

Client-safe delivery folder (Northstar → training client via `learner@example.com`):

```text
/northstar-fall-stills/
  01-masters/ (PNG/TIFF graded)
  02-exports/ social · thumb · print
  03-prompts-locks/ (txt of locks used)
  04-rights-note.md
  README.txt — what’s final vs alt
```

Include: file naming, color space, AI-assist disclosure if required, revision policy (what’s in-scope).

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If the client can’t find the print file, you didn’t deliver.”

*(Beat 2)* “Prompts and locks in the pack save Monday’s revision spiral.”

### Worked micro-example

Zip with masters, exports, locks, rights note → email fictional `learner@example.com`.

## Practice

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Ship masters, exports, locks, and a rights/README note—not a messy download pile.
