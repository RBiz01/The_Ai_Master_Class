## Beat sheet

1. **Cold open** — show the vague ask (30s).
2. **Lock decisions** — brief, style, subject/product, do-nots (90s).
3. **Run the prompt** — narrate output shape (120s).
4. **Score it** — mush / extras / off-brand + rubric (60s).
5. **Ship** — what goes to the delivery pack (45s).
