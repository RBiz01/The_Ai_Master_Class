## Teaching

### Speak camera without a vendor menu

You don’t need a specific app’s “cinematic” toggle. Portable language:

- **Focal feel:** 35mm environmental · 50mm natural · 85mm portrait compression  
- **DOF:** shallow subject separation · deep product tabletop  
- **Angles:** eye-level, three-quarter, top-down pack shot, low hero  
- **Light:** soft window, hard sun, overcast, rim/hair, practical lamps, bounce fill  

```text
Camera: 85mm feel · three-quarter · eye-level · shallow DOF
Light: soft window key camera-left · gentle fill · amber rim camera-right
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Ambiguous ‘cinematic lighting’ is a coin flip. Named key and rim is a recipe.”

*(Beat 2)* “Northstar product heroes: 50–85mm feel, three-quarter, soft key, amber rim.”

### Worked micro-example

**Vague:** “dramatic lighting.”  
**Controlled:** “hard side key, deep shadow, single amber practical, no fill.”

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Name focal feel, angle, DOF, and key/fill/rim—drop vague cinematic as your only light note.
