## Teaching

### Compose for the destination, not the preview

Stills for social, thumbnails, and ads need **negative space** on purpose. Northstar briefs call the empty zone:

- Where headline/type will sit  
- Where UI chrome or platform safe areas clip  
- Where the logo badge lives  

```text
Composition: subject right third · negative space left for headline
Safe margins: 8% from edges · no critical detail in platform chrome
Motif: soft amber orb in negative space (optional, low opacity)
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If the bottle fills the whole frame, design has nowhere to work.”

*(Beat 2)* “Negative space is a brand asset—treat it like a prop.”

### Worked micro-example

**Crowded:** centered bottle touching all edges.  
**Ship:** bottle on right third, clean left field for “Northstar · Fall Kit” type.
