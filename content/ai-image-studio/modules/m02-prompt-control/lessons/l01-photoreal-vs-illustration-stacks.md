## Teaching

### Pick a medium stack—then stay there

Mixing “photoreal” and “vector illustration” in one prompt creates mush hybrids. Northstar runs two stacks:

**Photoreal stack** — materials, camera, light, grade, skin/product realism limits.  
**Illustration stack** — line weight, flat vs textured fill, poster/editorial motif, limited palette.

```text
STACK: photoreal
materials + micro-detail + camera + light + grade
avoid: cartoon, vector, plastic CGI skin

STACK: illustration
line weight + fill style + poster motif + limited palette
avoid: photoreal pores, lensflare spam
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Medium is a decision, not a vibe.”

*(Beat 2)* “Campaign sets die when frame one is photo and frame three is sticker art.”

### Worked micro-example

**Bad:** “photoreal vector illustration of bottle.”  
**Good:** choose STACK photoreal + ceramic materials + 85mm feel.
