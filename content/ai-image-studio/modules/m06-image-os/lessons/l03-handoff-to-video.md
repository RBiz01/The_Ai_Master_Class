## Teaching

### Stills OS feeds video; it doesn't become video class

This course stays **stills-only**. Handoff to video means exporting clarity:

- Approved stills (heroes + alts)  
- Style / character / product locks  
- Palette + motif notes  
- Shot intents that could become motion later  
- Rights note  

```text
HANDOFF → video (not produced here)
Stills kit zip + locks txt + rights note
Intent: “Nova bottle hero may become 3s push-in later”
Do not: invent video prompts in this course pack
```

Differentiate from `generative-media`: they survey image+video; you hand off clean stills.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Good stills handoff saves the motion team a week of re-locking.”

*(Beat 2)* “You’re done when video can start without guessing brand.”

### Worked micro-example

Deliver 6 stills + locks + “optional push-in on hero” note—no video edit required.

## Practice

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Hand off approved stills, locks, and rights—not a half-baked video attempt.
