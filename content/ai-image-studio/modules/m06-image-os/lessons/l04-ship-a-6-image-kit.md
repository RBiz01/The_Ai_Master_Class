## Teaching

### Capstone: six that belong together

Final kit checklist:

1. Brief + style/character/product locks frozen  
2. Six shots from the batch list (hero → utility)  
3. Set QA + rubric pass  
4. Finish: inpaint/upscale as needed  
5. Exports for social/thumb/print where relevant  
6. Delivery folder + rights note to `learner@example.com`  

```text
KIT — Northstar 6
1 Hero bottle
2 Lid detail
3 Lifestyle tabletop
4 Nova with pin (fictional)
5 Social type-safe
6 Thumbnail punch
All: locks · rubric · rights · exports
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Shipping is a checklist, not a feeling.”

*(Beat 2)* “You now have an Image OS for stills—deeper than a survey, ready for campaigns.”

### Worked micro-example

One zip, six coherent stills, locks, rubric sheet, rights note—done.
