## Teaching

### Usable beats pretty

At **Northstar Studio**, creative lead Mira opens every stills job with a one-page brief—not a moodboard dump.

A usable still answers five questions before generation:

1. **Job** — hero product, social post, thumbnail, print leave-behind?
2. **Subject** — what must read at a glance (product silhouette, character face, logo mark)?
3. **Frame** — aspect ratio and safe crop for the destination.
4. **Brand fit** — palette, motif, tone locked to the kit.
5. **Pass/fail** — what would make Legal or Brand reject it.

```text
Still brief for Northstar Studio
Use case: [hero / social / thumb / print]
Subject must read: […]
Aspect + safe crop: […]
Brand locks: palette · motif · type mood
Reject if: mush detail · extra limbs · off-palette · real-person likeness
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If you can’t say what ‘done’ looks like, the model will invent pretty noise.”

*(Beat 2)* “Northstar’s amber-to-magenta kit isn’t a suggestion—it’s a reject filter.”

### Worked micro-example

**Vague:** “Cool bottle shot for Instagram.”  
**Usable:** Square 1:1 hero of Northstar ceramic bottle, three-quarter front, soft window key, amber rim light, magenta accent gel on backdrop, logo readable, no extra props, reject plastic skin and floating labels.
