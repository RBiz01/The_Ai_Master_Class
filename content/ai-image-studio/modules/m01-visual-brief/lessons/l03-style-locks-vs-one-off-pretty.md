## Teaching

### Locks ship campaigns; pretty ships screenshots

A **style lock** is a short, reusable block: medium, palette, motif, lens feel, grade, and hard do-nots. A **one-off pretty** prompt is a pile of adjectives that never transfer to image 2–6.

Northstar Studio style lock (stills):

```text
STYLE LOCK — Northstar Studio stills
Medium: photoreal product / lifestyle still
Palette: amber #f59e0b · magenta #ec4899 · charcoal #0b0f19
Motif: soft orb glow, clean negative space, ceramic/matte materials
Lens: 50–85mm feel, controlled DOF
Grade: warm highlights, magenta-leaning shadows, no plastic HDR
Do-not: real-person likeness, NSFW, busy clutter, extra fingers/limbs, off-palette neon
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If image two looks like a different brand, you never had a lock—you had a lucky render.”

*(Beat 2)* “Paste the lock every time. Change only subject and shot.”

### Worked micro-example

**One-off:** “beautiful cinematic stunning product photo masterpiece.”  
**Lock:** paste STYLE LOCK + “Northstar bottle, three-quarter, tabletop.”

## Takeaway

Ship a reusable style lock; treat pretty adjectives as optional spice, not the system.
