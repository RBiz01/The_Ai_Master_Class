## Teaching

### Four dials that survive any model

Model-agnostic control starts with four dials every Midjourney / Flux / SD-style stack understands:

| Dial | Lock language | Example (Northstar) |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| Subject | noun + materials + pose/angle | ceramic bottle, matte glaze, 3/4 front |
| Light | key + fill + rim + quality | soft window key, gentle fill, amber rim |
| Lens | focal feel + DOF | 85mm portrait feel, shallow DOF |
| Grade | palette + contrast + film mood | warm amber midtones, magenta shadows, clean contrast |

```text
Subject: […]
Light: key=…; fill=…; rim=…; quality=soft|hard
Lens: focal feel=…; DOF=shallow|deep
Grade: palette=amber/magenta; contrast=clean; avoid plastic HDR
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Subject without light is a catalog cutout. Light without lens is a mood without sharpness.”

*(Beat 2)* “Grade is brand memory—Northstar’s amber key and magenta shadow should survive every export.”

### Worked micro-example

**Stack:** ceramic bottle · soft window key · 85mm feel · amber/magenta grade → consistent hero stills across tools.
