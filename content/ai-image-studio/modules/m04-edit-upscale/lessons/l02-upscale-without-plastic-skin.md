## Teaching

### Sharpen truth, don't invent porcelain

Aggressive upscalers invent pore-spam and plastic ceramic. Northstar finishing:

- Prefer moderate scale steps (e.g. 2×) over giant jumps  
- Protect faces/hands and logo regions—mask if needed  
- Dial down “beauty” / skin-smooth sliders  
- Compare 100% crops before/after  

```text
Upscale pass
Scale: 2× (repeat if needed)
Protect: face · hands · logo
Avoid: plastic skin · halo crunch · fake fabric weave
QA: 100% crop on eyes/logo/edges
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If it looks like a phone beauty filter, you over-upscaled.”

*(Beat 2)* “Clients print large—plastic glaze shows.”

### Worked micro-example

**Bad:** 4× with max denoise → porcelain bottle, melting type.  
**Good:** 2×, protect logo, light sharpen, crop QA.

## Takeaway

Upscale in moderate steps, protect critical regions, and QA 100% crops for plastic artifacts.
