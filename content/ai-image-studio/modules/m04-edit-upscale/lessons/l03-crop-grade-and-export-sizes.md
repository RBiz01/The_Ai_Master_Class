## Teaching

### Export is part of the creative

Northstar delivery matrix (stills):

| Destination | Aspect | Notes |
|-------------|--------|-------|
| Social feed | 1:1 or 4:5 | Leave type-safe margins |
| Thumbnail | 16:9 | Face/product larger; less neg space |
| Story/vertical | 9:16 | Top/bottom chrome safe |
| Print leave-behind | 300dpi, bleed | Flatten grade; soft-proof |

Grade last: amber highlights, magenta-leaning shadows, clean contrast—no plastic HDR.

```text
Export pack
1:1 · 4:5 · 16:9 · 9:16 (+ print TIFF/PNG as needed)
Grade: Northstar amber/magenta · verify logo legibility
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “A perfect square that dies in 9:16 was never finished.”

*(Beat 2)* “Grade once on the master; derive crops—don’t re-grade every size by eye.”

### Worked micro-example

Master graded → derive 1:1 social + 16:9 thumb with safe crops.
