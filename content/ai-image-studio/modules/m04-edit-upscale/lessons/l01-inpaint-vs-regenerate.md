## Teaching

### Local fault → inpaint; concept miss → regenerate

**Inpaint** when composition, light, and brand read are good but a region fails (logo mush, stray prop, hand glitch). **Regenerate** when subject identity, pose, or palette is wrong—patching will fight the frame.

```text
INPAINT if: local mush/extra · rest of frame on-brief
REGENERATE if: wrong subject · off-palette · bad pose/composition
Always: re-apply style + product/character locks after either path
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Don’t inpaint a bad idea. Don’t regenerate a one-pixel logo smear.”

*(Beat 2)* “Northstar rule: if Brand would reject the whole mood, regenerate.”

### Worked micro-example

**Inpaint:** warped wordmark on an otherwise perfect bottle.  
**Regenerate:** bottle is glossy plastic and blue—wrong SKU.
