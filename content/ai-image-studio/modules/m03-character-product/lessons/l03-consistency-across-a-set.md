## Teaching

### QA the set, not the single hero

Ship sets, not orphans. After generating 4–6 Northstar stills, compare side-by-side:

1. Character traits / product silhouette match  
2. Palette and motif within lock  
3. Light direction family similar enough  
4. No extras or mush on hero details  
5. Crop language coherent for the channel  

```text
SET QA (6 stills)
Pass if: same hero/SKU · palette within lock · light family coherent
Fail if: trait drift · off-palette · extras · mismatched lens feel
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “One perfect frame and five cousins is not a kit.”

*(Beat 2)* “Pull the weakest two; regenerate with the same locks—not new vibes.”

### Worked micro-example

**Fail set:** three lids, two hair colors, one off-palette backdrop.  
**Fix:** re-lock sheet + product; regenerate outliers.
