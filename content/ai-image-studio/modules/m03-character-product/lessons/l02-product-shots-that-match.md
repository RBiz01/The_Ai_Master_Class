## Teaching

### SKU truth over artistic drift

Product stills fail when the bottle grows a new lid or the logo slides. Northstar product lock:

```text
PRODUCT LOCK — Northstar Ceramic Bottle
Silhouette: tall cylinder, soft shoulder, flat matte lid
Material: matte ceramic, subtle speckled glaze
Logo: centered wordmark, small, high-contrast
Colorway: charcoal body · amber lip ring · magenta stamp on base
Do-not: extra labels · warped logo · wrong lid · competitor marks
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Marketing can stylize light—not invent a second product.”

*(Beat 2)* “Match silhouette and logo placement before you chase mood.”

### Worked micro-example

**Drift:** glossy plastic bottle, huge logo, blue ring.  
**Match:** matte ceramic, small centered wordmark, amber lip ring.

## Takeaway

Lock silhouette, materials, logo placement, and colorway—reject artistic SKU drift.
