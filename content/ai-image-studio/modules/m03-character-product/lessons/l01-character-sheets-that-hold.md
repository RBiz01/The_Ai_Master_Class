## Teaching

### Traits transfer; vibes don't

Northstar uses **fictional** talent only—no real celebrity likeness. A sheet that holds lists countable traits:

- Face shape, hair, age band, wardrobe colors  
- Signature prop or motif  
- Expression range allowed  
- Hard do-nots (no real lookalikes)

```text
CHARACTER SHEET — “Nova” (fictional)
Hair: short black with amber tips · Face: oval, soft jaw
Wardrobe: charcoal jacket, magenta stitch accent
Prop: ceramic Northstar pin
Expression: calm confidence · no caricature grin
Do-not: real-person likeness · NSFW · extra fingers
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If you can’t checklist the traits, you can’t QA the set.”

*(Beat 2)* “Fictional Nova stays Nova—amber tips and magenta stitch are the memory.”

### Worked micro-example

**Weak:** “cool stylish person.”  
**Hold:** paste CHARACTER SHEET + shot note.

## Takeaway

Lock countable traits on a fictional sheet; ban real-person likeness explicitly.
