## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Acme Ops needs a handbook assistant for escalation windows, vendor onboarding steps, and portal go-live checklists—not open-world chat.

### Beat 2

Success: correct cited answers on a golden set; polite refusal when docs don’t cover it; no invented policy numbers.

### Beat 3

Scope v1: PDF handbook + 3 SOPs. Out of scope: payroll, legal advice, live PII lookups.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)
