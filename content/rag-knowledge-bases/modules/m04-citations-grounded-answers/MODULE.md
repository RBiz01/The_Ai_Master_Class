# Module 4 — Citations & grounded answers

**Slug:** `m04-citations-grounded-answers`  
**Course:** RAG & Knowledge Bases for Operators  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Teach patterns that earn operator trust: cite sources, ground claims in retrieved text, refuse when evidence is weak, and design UIs people actually use.

## Why this module ships here

Without citations and refusals, a knowledge assistant is just a confident guesser wearing a handbook costume.
