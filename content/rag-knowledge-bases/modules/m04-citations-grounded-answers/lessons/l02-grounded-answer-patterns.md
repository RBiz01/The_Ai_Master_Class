## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Pattern: evidence snippet → plain-language claim → source ID.

### Beat 2

Ban unsupported extras: no inventing severity codes not in the chunk.

### Beat 3

Multiple claims need multiple citations when they come from different sections.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“Pattern: evidence snippet → plain-language claim → source ID.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Ban unsupported extras: no inventing severity codes not in the chunk.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done with this lesson when you can apply this on an Acme Ops handbook scenario without guessing.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> You should escalate fast and also page legal immediately.

**After**

> Per Handbook §3.1, escalate P1 within 4 hours to the on-call lead. Legal paging is not covered in retrieved sections—do not invent it.
