# Lesson 4.3 — Refusal when retrieval is weak

**Module:** Citations & grounded answers  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Refuse or ask clarifying questions when top passages don’t support an answer.

## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Weak retrieval > wrong answer. Teach the model to say ‘not in the handbook.’

### Beat 2

Offer next steps: clarify question, ping owner, or open the source doc.

### Beat 3

Acme Ops: payroll and medical advice are out of scope—hard refuse.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“Weak retrieval > wrong answer. Teach the model to say ‘not in the handbook.’”

*(Beat 2)*

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done with this lesson when you can apply this on an Acme Ops handbook scenario without guessing.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Guess the vacation accrual when the handbook chunk is about escalations.

**After**

> I don’t have an approved handbook passage on vacation accrual in this index. Try HR SOP or ask people-ops@example.com (fictional).
