## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Every source has an owner and a refresh SLA (e.g. reindex within 24h of handbook publish).

### Beat 2

Access: only people who can read the doc can query it; drafts stay out of prod.

### Beat 3

Acme Ops: handbook owner = Ops Lead; assistant owner = Automation PM (fictional roles).

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“Every source has an owner and a refresh SLA (e.g. reindex within 24h of handbook publish).”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Access: only people who can read the doc can query it; drafts stay out of prod.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done with this lesson when you can apply this on an Acme Ops handbook scenario without guessing.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Anyone can upload anything; reindex whenever someone remembers.

**After**

> Approved sources only; owner + version badge; nightly reindex; draft index isolated.
