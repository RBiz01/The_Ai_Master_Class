## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Monday: check index freshness, fail rates, top unanswered questions.

### Beat 2

Publish a short changelog when policies change—operators hate silent shifts.

### Beat 3

Keep a human escalation path: ‘talk to Ops Lead’ when the bot refuses.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“Monday: check index freshness, fail rates, top unanswered questions.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Publish a short changelog when policies change—operators hate silent shifts.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done with this lesson when you can apply this on an Acme Ops handbook scenario without guessing.”
