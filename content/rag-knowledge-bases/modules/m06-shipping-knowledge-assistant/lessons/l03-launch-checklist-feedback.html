Lesson 6.3 — Launch checklist & feedback loops

Module: Shipping a team knowledge assistant Duration: ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive Interactive: l03-interactive.html

Learning objective

Run a go-live checklist and wire feedback into weekly golden-set updates.

What to do

  1. Watch the Lesson video once for the visual hook.
  2. Read Teaching (and the worked example) without rushing.
  3. Open l03-interactive.html and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Launch checklist & feedback loops).
  4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
  5. You’re done when: you can explain — in plain language — Run a go-live checklist and wire feedback into weekly golden-set updates. and l03-interactive.html shows success.

Teaching

The Monday-morning problem

At Acme Ops, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

Beat 1

Checklist: golden-set green, citations on, refuse paths tested, access reviewed, rollback plan.

Beat 2

In-product feedback → triage queue → eval items → fix → note in changelog.

Beat 3

Acme Ops soft launch: 10 operators for 5 days before wider rollout.

Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

(Beat 1) “Checklist: golden-set green, citations on, refuse paths tested, access reviewed, rollback plan.”

(Beat 2) “In-product feedback → triage queue → eval items → fix → note in changelog.”

(Beat 3) “You’re done with this lesson when you can apply this on an Acme Ops handbook scenario without guessing.”

Worked micro-example

Before

Ship Friday night with no rollback.

After

Soft launch Mon; kill switch disables writes to index; daily triage of thumbs-down.

Practice

Open l03-interactive.html and complete the drill until the checker lights green.

Takeaway

Launch checklist & feedback loops — practice it on the fictional Acme Ops handbook before you touch production docs. No real secrets or PII.
