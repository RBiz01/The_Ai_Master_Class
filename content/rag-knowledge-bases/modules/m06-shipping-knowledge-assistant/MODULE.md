## Goal

Ship a scoped Acme Ops knowledge assistant: ownership, access, freshness, launch checklist, feedback loops, and Monday operating habits.

## Why this module ships here

A great RAG demo that never launches helps no one. Module 6 turns the stack into a trusted teammate.
