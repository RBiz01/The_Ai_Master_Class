# Lesson 2.1 — What a chunk is (and isn’t)

**Module:** Chunking & document prep  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Define chunks as retrieval units—not random character slices—and see why boundaries matter.

## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

A chunk is the unit you retrieve and cite. Too big → noisy context. Too small → lost meaning.

### Beat 2

Prefer semantic boundaries: headings, procedures, FAQ pairs—not mid-sentence cuts.

### Beat 3

Acme Ops escalation section should stay intact enough that ‘4-hour P1 window’ stays with its conditions.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Split the handbook every 500 characters with no regard for headings.

**After**

> Chunk by H2 sections with ~400–800 tokens; keep procedure steps together; store heading path as metadata.
