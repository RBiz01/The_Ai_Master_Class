## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Tables often serialize as soup. Prefer row-wise or key-value chunks for SLA grids.

### Beat 2

Multi-column PDFs and scanned pages need OCR QA—don’t silently index gibberish.

### Beat 3

Acme Ops vendor SLA table: each row becomes a mini-chunk with vendor + metric + threshold.

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Paste the whole SLA table as one unreadable string into a single chunk.

**After**

> One chunk per SLA row: ‘Vendor Northwind · P1 response ≤ 1h · Source: Handbook §4.2’.
