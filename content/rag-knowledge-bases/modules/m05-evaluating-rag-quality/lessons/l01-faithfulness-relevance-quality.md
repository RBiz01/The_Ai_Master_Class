## Teaching

### The Monday-morning problem

At **Acme Ops**, teams need answers grounded in the handbook—not confident guesses. This lesson locks a skill you’ll reuse all week.

### Beat 1

Faithfulness: does the answer stick to retrieved text? Relevance: are the passages on-topic?

### Beat 2

Answer quality: clarity, completeness, actionability for standup.

### Beat 3

Acme Ops can pass relevance and still fail faithfulness if it invents a 2-hour window.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)
