## Who this is for

Marketers, founders, designers, and creative operators who need **on-brand stills and short-form video** without booking a studio every week. You already dabble with image models—now you want repeatable pipelines, brand consistency, and client-safe delivery.

Comfortable with chat UIs and basic design tools helps. No 3D suite or cinema camera required.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Read and write **visual prompts** with subject, scene, camera, lighting, and style locks.
2. Build **character and brand kits** that stay consistent across scenes.
3. Run **edit · upscale · cleanup** passes that survive client zoom-ins.
4. Ship **short-form AI video** from storyboard → motion → captioned export.
5. Navigate **rights, ethics, and disclosure** for commercial delivery.
6. Complete a **Northstar Studio portfolio sprint** with stills + a short cut.

## Prerequisites

- Access to at least one modern image model and one image-to-video (or similar) tool
- A fictional or practice brand brief (we use **Northstar Studio** throughout)
- Intermediate comfort iterating on creative feedback

### Module 1 — Visual literacy for generative tools *(built)*

1. How generative models read a visual brief  
2. Subject, scene, and camera language  
3. Reading references like a creative director  
4. Spotting artifacts and AI tells  

### Module 2 — Prompting for style, lighting, and composition *(built)*

1. Style anchors that stick  
2. Lighting recipes that sell the mood  
3. Composition grids and negative space  
4. Prompt stacks for brandable looks  

### Module 3 — Character & brand consistency *(built)*

1. Character sheets that transfer  
2. Brand kits: color, type, motif  
3. Seed, reference, and lock strategies  
4. Multi-scene consistency checklists  

### Module 4 — Editing, upscaling, and cleanup *(built)*

1. Inpaint and outpaint with intent  
2. Upscale without mush  
3. Cleanup passes for delivery

### Module 5 — Short-form AI video pipelines *(built)*

2. Image-to-video and motion prompts
