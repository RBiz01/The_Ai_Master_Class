## Teaching

### Motion prompt pattern

`camera: slow push-in; subject: static lamp; ambience: dust motes in window light; avoid: morphing metal, warping logo area`

### Safe moves

- Slow push / pull  
- Parallax on background  
- Gentle light flicker on practicals  

### Risky moves

- Orbiting too fast  
- “Transform into …”  
- Hands appearing from nowhere  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Motion should sell the still—not reinvent the product.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Make the lamp dance and morph.

**After**

> Slow push-in on locked Northstar lamp still; static subject; soft dust motes; no morphing metal.
