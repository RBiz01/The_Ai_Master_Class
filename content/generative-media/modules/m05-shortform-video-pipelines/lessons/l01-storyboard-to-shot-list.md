# Lesson 5.1 — Storyboard to shot list

**Module:** Short-form AI video pipelines  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Convert a 15s Northstar teaser into 4–6 shots with purpose.

## Lesson video

![Lesson video](assets/l01-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l01-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### 15s teaser skeleton

1. Hook still with micro-push (0–3s)  
2. Detail insert (3–6s)  
3. Lifestyle / desk context (6–10s)  
4. Brand end card (10–15s)  

Shot list columns: **# · visual · motion · VO/caption · duration**

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If you can’t list the shots, you’re hoping the model directs for you.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Make a cool video of the lamp.

**After**

> 4-shot list: hook push on lamp → shade detail → desk context → pink→amber end card with Northstar wordmark.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

## Takeaway

Storyboard → shot list before motion prompts.
