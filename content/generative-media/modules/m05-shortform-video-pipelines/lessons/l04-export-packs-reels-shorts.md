## Teaching

### Pack

| Ratio | Use |
|-------|-----|
| 9:16 | Reels / Shorts / TikTok primary |
| 1:1 | Feed fallback |
| 16:9 | Site / YouTube crop |

Keep UI-safe margins; end card last 2s with Northstar wordmark.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Design vertical-first, crop out—not the other way around.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Export only 16:9 and stretch it.

**After**

> Master 9:16 with safe margins + end card; derive 1:1 and 16:9 crops.
