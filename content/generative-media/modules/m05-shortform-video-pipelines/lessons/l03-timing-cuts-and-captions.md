## Teaching

### Timing rules of thumb

- Hook claim in first 2s (on-screen text)  
- Cut every 2–4s unless motion is strong  
- Captions ≤ 6 words per flash when possible  

Northstar hook example: **“Studio light. Zero studio.”**

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Most feeds start muted. Captions carry the hook.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Long VO, tiny captions later.

**After**

> 0–2s big caption hook; cuts ~3s; readable captions throughout; end card CTA.
