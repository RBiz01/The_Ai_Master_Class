**Module:** Editing, upscaling, and cleanup

## Learning objective

Upscale for delivery while avoiding plastic skin and swimming wood grain.

## Teaching

### Upscale checklist

- Start from best base frame (not a muddy reject)  
- Moderate scale (2× often safer than 8× fantasy)  
- Compare at 100% and 200%  
- Re-detail edges if tool softens metal  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Bigger isn’t better if materials turn to pudding.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> 8× upscale everything, max creativity.

**After**

> 2× upscale from clean base; check brass edges at 200%; light detail pass if soft.
