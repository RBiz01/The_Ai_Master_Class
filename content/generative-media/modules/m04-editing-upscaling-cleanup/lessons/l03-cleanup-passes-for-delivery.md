## Teaching

### Delivery cleanup order

1. Spot heal dust / sensor-like blobs  
2. Micro-contrast on product edges  
3. Crop to export ratio  
4. Add **real** typography in layout tool—not AI gibberish  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Typography belongs in design tools. Models invent alphabets.”
