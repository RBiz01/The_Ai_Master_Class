# Lesson 4.1 — Inpaint and outpaint with intent

**Module:** Editing, upscaling, and cleanup  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Mask and rewrite only what should change—never “fix everything”.

![Lesson video](assets/l01-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l01-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Intent sentences

- **Inpaint:** “Replace melted label with blank matte brass plate—no text.”  
- **Outpaint:** “Extend charcoal backdrop upward for headline space; keep lamp untouched.”

### Rules

1. Mask tight  
2. Prompt only the change  
3. Preserve locked subject with low denoise / ref  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Inpaint is surgery. Don’t amputate the hero.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Fix the whole image.

**After**

> Inpaint only the melted label → blank matte plate; leave lamp geometry untouched.

## Takeaway

State the change; protect the hero.
