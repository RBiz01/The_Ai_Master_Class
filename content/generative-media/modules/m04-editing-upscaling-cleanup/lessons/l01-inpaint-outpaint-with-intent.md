# Lesson 4.1 — Inpaint and outpaint with intent

**Module:** Editing, upscaling, and cleanup  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Mask and rewrite only what should change—never “fix everything”.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once; pause only if a beat truly clicks for your role.
2. Work through **Teaching** and the worked example until you could explain it out loud.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Inpaint and outpaint with intent).
4. Jot one reusable line in your own words — something you’d actually use at work.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Mask and rewrite only what should change—never “fix everything”.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Intent sentences

- **Inpaint:** “Replace melted label with blank matte brass plate—no text.”  
- **Outpaint:** “Extend charcoal backdrop upward for headline space; keep lamp untouched.”

### Rules

1. Mask tight  
2. Prompt only the change  
3. Preserve locked subject with low denoise / ref  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Inpaint is surgery. Don’t amputate the hero.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Fix the whole image.

**After**

> Inpaint only the melted label → blank matte plate; leave lamp geometry untouched.

Open `l01-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

## Takeaway

State the change; protect the hero.
