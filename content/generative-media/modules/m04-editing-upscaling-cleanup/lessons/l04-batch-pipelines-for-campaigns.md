**Module:** Editing, upscaling, and cleanup  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive

## Learning objective

Describe a repeatable batch flow for a 6-still Northstar campaign.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Batch pipelines for campaigns).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Describe a repeatable batch flow for a 6-still Northstar campaign.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Pipeline

Brief → stack → generate variants → pick heroes → inpaint → upscale → cleanup → export pack (1:1, 4:5, 16:9)

Name files: `ns_lamp_hero_v3_45.png`

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Campaigns need pipes, not one-off genius.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Make six different cool images somehow.

**After**

> One stack → 6 angles → pick → inpaint/upscale/cleanup → export 1:1 + 4:5 + 16:9 with versioned filenames.

Open `l04-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

## Takeaway

Batch = same stack, managed variants, named exports.
