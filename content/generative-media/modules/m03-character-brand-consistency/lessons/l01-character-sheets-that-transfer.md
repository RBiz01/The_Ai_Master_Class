# Lesson 3.1 — Character sheets that transfer

**Module:** Character & brand consistency  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Write a classroom-safe character sheet for a fictional Northstar host character.

![Lesson video](assets/l01-video.mp4)

## Teaching

### Sheet fields that matter

- **Identity:** name, role (fictional)  
- **Face traits:** age range, hair, silhouette—not a real celebrity  
- **Wardrobe lock:** colors, fabrics, signature piece  
- **Do-nots:** no real-person likeness  

### Example — Mira (fictional host)

Mira, mid-30s creative director vibe, short black hair with soft side part, charcoal blazer, amber pocket square, calm expression, Northstar pin—**original character, not based on any real person**.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Consistency starts on paper before the first generation.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Make a woman who looks like [famous actor].

**After**

> Mira (original): mid-30s, short black hair, charcoal blazer, amber pocket square, calm expression—no celebrity likeness.

Original sheets only—never celebrity lookalikes.
