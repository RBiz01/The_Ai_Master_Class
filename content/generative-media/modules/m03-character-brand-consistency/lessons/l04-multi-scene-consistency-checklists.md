## Teaching

### Consistency board (print it)

- Wardrobe pieces match sheet  
- Palette within brand kit  
- Signature prop present (Northstar pin / lamp)  
- Lighting family related (not random genres)  
- No identity drift on Mira  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Campaigns are systems. Checklists catch drift before the client does.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Ship all three scenes—close enough.

**After**

> Board pass: wardrobe OK, palette OK, pin present in 3/3, lighting family = dawn desk. Scene 2 hair length drifted—regen with sheet.

Open `l04-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

## Takeaway

Multi-scene = checklist, not vibes.
