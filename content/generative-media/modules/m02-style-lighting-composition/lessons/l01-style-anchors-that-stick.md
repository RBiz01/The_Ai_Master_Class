## Teaching

### Anatomy of a style anchor

A reusable block (3–6 clauses):

1. Medium / fidelity — photoreal product still  
2. Grade — warm amber highlights, charcoal shadows  
3. Texture — subtle film grain, soft speculars  
4. Do-nots — no neon cyberpunk, no busy patterns  

### Northstar default anchor

> photoreal product photography, warm amber accent light, charcoal #0b0f19 grounds, subtle grain, clean commercial look, no neon, no glitch

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Style anchors are brand voice for pixels.”
