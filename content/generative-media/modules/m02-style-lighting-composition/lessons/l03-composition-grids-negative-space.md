## Teaching

### Composition for marketing stills

- **Rule of thirds** — subject on intersection  
- **Lower-third hero** — open sky/desk for headline  
- **Centered icon** — symmetrical product icons  
- **Leading lines** — desk edge guides eye to lamp  

### Negative space is a feature

Northstar leaves **30–40%** clean dark space for type. Prompt it: “subject lower-left, large empty charcoal negative space upper-right.”

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Pretty isn’t usable. Usable leaves room for the headline.”
