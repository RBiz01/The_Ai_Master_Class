Lesson 2.2 — Lighting recipes that sell the mood

Module: Prompting for style, lighting, and composition Duration: ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive Interactive: l02-interactive.html

Learning objective

Specify key, fill, and rim lighting so mood becomes reproducible.

What to do

  1. Cue up the Lesson video and watch it once before you dig into the text.
  2. Read Teaching carefully — especially the worked example you can reuse Monday.
  3. Open l02-interactive.html and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Lighting recipes that sell the mood).
  4. Apply the idea once in your words (sticky note or note app is fine).
  5. You’re done when: you can explain — in plain language — Specify key, fill, and rim lighting so mood becomes reproducible. and l02-interactive.html shows success.

Teaching

Three lights, one sentence

RoleJobPrompt cue
KeyShape the subjectsoft window key from camera-left
FillLift shadowsgentle fill, low ratio
RimSeparate from bgwarm amber rim from behind-right

Recipes Northstar uses

  • Dawn desk: soft cool window key left + warm amber practical lamp
  • Hero product: softbox key 45° + weak fill + bright rim
  • Moody teaser: hard key, almost no fill, colored rim

Talk-over narration

(Beat 1) “If you only say cinematic lighting, the model picks a random movie.”

Worked micro-example

Before

Cinematic lighting please.

After

Soft window key from camera-left, gentle fill, warm amber rim from behind-right, photoreal desk lamp hero.

Practice

Open l02-interactive.html and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

Takeaway

Key · fill · rim beats “cinematic” every time.
