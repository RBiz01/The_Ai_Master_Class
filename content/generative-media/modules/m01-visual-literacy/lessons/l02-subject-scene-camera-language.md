## Learning objective

Use precise subject/scene/camera vocabulary so the model stops inventing props, places, and lenses.

## Teaching

### Lexicon that ships

Northstar Studio keeps a wall card:

| Layer | Ask | Example tokens |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| Subject | What is the hero? | brass desk lamp, matte ceramic mug |
| Scene | Where does it live? | walnut desk, dawn office, seamless slate |
| Camera | How do we see it? | 50mm, eye-level, 3/4, shallow DOF |

### Camera language cheat sheet

- **Angle:** eye-level · low · high · top-down · 3/4  
- **Lens feel:** 24mm wide · 50mm natural · 85mm portrait compression  
- **DOF:** deep (everything sharp) · shallow (subject pop)  
- **Framing:** centered · rule-of-thirds · negative space left/right for type  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Adjectives without nouns are mood boards. Nouns with camera language are briefs.”
