## Learning objective

Deconstruct a reference still into transferable locks (palette, light direction, props, composition) without copying protected IP.

## Teaching

### Reference ≠ remix illegally

At Northstar Studio we **study** references; we do not paste a celebrity portrait or a protected campaign and ask to “make it the same.”

### CD checklist (60 seconds)

1. **Palette:** dominant + accent + background  
2. **Light direction:** key from left/right, hard/soft, rim?  
3. **Composition:** where is the mass? negative space?  
4. **Props / materials:** metal, wood, fabric—name finishes  
5. **What we will NOT copy:** logos, faces of real people, trademarked characters  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “A good CD steals structure, not identity.”
