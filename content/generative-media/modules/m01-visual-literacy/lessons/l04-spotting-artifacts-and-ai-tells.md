## Teaching

### The 90-second QA pass

Before anyone says “ship it”:

1. **Edges** — hair, glass, lamp shade: clean or melted?  
2. **Text & logos** — gibberish lettering? invent a fake mark?  
3. **Symmetry & count** — extra knobs, warped screws, weird reflections  
4. **Materials** — plastic looking like metal? wood grain swimming?  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Clients zoom. Artifacts hide at 50% and scream at 200%.”
