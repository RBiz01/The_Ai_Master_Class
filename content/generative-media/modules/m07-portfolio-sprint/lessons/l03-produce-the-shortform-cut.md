# Lesson 7.3 — Produce the short-form cut

**Module:** Portfolio project sprint  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

## Learning objective

Build the 15s 9:16 teaser from shot list through captions and end card.

![Lesson video](assets/l03-video.mp4)

## Teaching

### Cut checklist

- Shot list followed  
- Motion prompts safe (no morph)  
- Captions muted-first  
- End card 2s with wordmark  
- Export pack ready  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “The cut is where the pipeline becomes a story.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Stitch random clips with no captions.

**After**

> 15s 9:16: hook caption, 4 shots, safe motion, end card, export pack.

## Practice

Open `l03-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

## Takeaway

Shot list + captions + end card = shippable cut.
