# Lesson 7.1 — Brief intake and creative strategy

**Module:** Portfolio project sprint  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Turn a Northstar launch brief into goals, audience, visual thesis, and success metrics.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once; pause only if a beat truly clicks for your role.
2. Work through **Teaching** and the worked example until you could explain it out loud.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Brief intake and creative strategy).
4. Jot one reusable line in your own words — something you’d actually use at work.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Turn a Northstar launch brief into goals, audience, visual thesis, and success metrics.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Intake questions

- Who scrolls past?  
- What must they feel in 2 seconds?  
- Still count + video length?  
- Must-use brand locks?  
- Rights constraints?  

### Strategy one-pager

**Thesis:** “Warm desk craft, zero studio friction.”  
**Look:** pink→amber accents on charcoal; lamp hero; Mira optional.  
**Proof:** 3 hero stills + 15s 9:16 teaser.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Strategy is the brief you give yourself before the model.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> We’ll just generate stuff until marketing likes one.

**After**

> One-pager: audience, thesis, look locks, deliverables (3 stills + 15s), rights notes.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.
