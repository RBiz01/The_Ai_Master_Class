## Teaching

### Disclosure snippet (adapt to jurisdiction)

“Visuals produced with AI-assisted tools under Northstar Studio direction; final art directed and QA’d by humans.”

Contracts should cover: ownership of finals, tool list, likeness warranties, revision rounds.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Disclosure builds trust. Hiding process burns it.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Don’t tell the client we used AI.

**After**

> Include AI-assisted disclosure + tool list + human art direction note in the SOW.
