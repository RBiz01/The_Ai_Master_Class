## Learning objective

Assemble a client folder: masters, crops, captions, license memo, QA notes.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Delivery packages Northstar Studio ships).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Assemble a client folder: masters, crops, captions, license memo, QA notes.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.
