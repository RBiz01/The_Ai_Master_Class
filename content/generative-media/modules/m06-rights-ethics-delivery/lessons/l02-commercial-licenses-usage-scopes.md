**Module:** Rights, ethics, and client delivery

## Learning objective

Match tool licenses and stock/model terms to the client’s usage scope.

1. Cue up the **Lesson video** and watch it once before you dig into the text.
2. Read **Teaching** carefully — especially the worked example you can reuse Monday.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Commercial licenses and usage scopes).

5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Match tool licenses and stock/model terms to the client’s usage scope.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Scope questions

- Paid social?  
- Out-of-home?  
- Resale as templates?  
- How long? which territories?  

Document the **tool plan + license notes** in the delivery memo.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Usage scope is part of the creative brief—not an afterthought.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> We generated it so we own everything forever worldwide.

**After**

> Confirm tool commercial terms; note usage: paid social, 12 months, US/CA; no resale templates.

Open `l02-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

## Takeaway

Write the usage scope down.
