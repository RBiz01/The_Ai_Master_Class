## Teaching

### Hard rules (classroom + client)

- No celebrity lookalikes  
- No real client employees without written consent  
- Prefer products, spaces, and **original** fictional characters (Mira)  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If you wouldn’t cast them without a release, don’t prompt them.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Make the CEO look like a famous actor.

**After**

> Use original character Mira—or product-only hero—with documented consent for any real staff portraits.

## Takeaway

No celebrity likenesses. Consent for real people.
