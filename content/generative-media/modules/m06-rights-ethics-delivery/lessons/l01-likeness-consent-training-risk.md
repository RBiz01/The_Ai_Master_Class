# Lesson 6.1 — Likeness, consent, and training-data risk

**Module:** Rights, ethics, and client delivery  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Avoid celebrity likenesses and unauthorized real-person faces in commercial work.

## Lesson video

![Lesson video](assets/l01-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l01-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Hard rules (classroom + client)

- No celebrity lookalikes  
- No real client employees without written consent  
- Prefer products, spaces, and **original** fictional characters (Mira)  

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If you wouldn’t cast them without a release, don’t prompt them.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Make the CEO look like a famous actor.

**After**

> Use original character Mira—or product-only hero—with documented consent for any real staff portraits.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and complete the drill until success criteria turn green.

## Takeaway

No celebrity likenesses. Consent for real people.
