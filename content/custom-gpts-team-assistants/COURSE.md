## Who this is for

Ops leads, PMs, support managers, founders, and anyone who has outgrown one-off chat prompts and wants **shareable assistants** teammates will actually open—Custom GPTs, Claude Projects, Gemini Gems, and similar shells.

No engineering team required. Patterns stay **model-agnostic**. All scenarios use fictional **Acme Ops** assistants.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Map the **anatomy** of a custom assistant (instructions, knowledge, actions, starters, sharing).
2. Write **instructions that stick**: role, mission, non-goals, hard rules, output contracts.
3. Curate **knowledge packs** with freshness, refuse/escalate/cite boundaries, and least privilege.
4. Add **light actions** safely—or keep draft-only when actions do not earn the complexity.
5. **Version and eval** assistants with golden sets, regression, and rollback playbooks.
6. Run **team rollout & governance**: sharing, ownership, adoption, policy, and sunset.

## Prerequisites

- Access to any custom-assistant shell (Custom GPT / Project / Gem or equivalent)
- A real workflow you want to productize (we use fictional **Acme Ops**)
- Comfort writing clear prompts (Prompt Engineering Mastery helps but is not required)
