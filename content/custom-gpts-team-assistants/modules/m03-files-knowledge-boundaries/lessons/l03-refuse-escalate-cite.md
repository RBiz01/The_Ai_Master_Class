# Lesson 3.3 — Boundaries: refuse, escalate, cite

**Module:** Files, knowledge & boundaries  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4–8 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Teach the assistant when to refuse, when to escalate to a human, and when to cite knowledge.

![Lesson video](assets/l03-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l03-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Beat 1

Refuse: illegal, unsafe, or out-of-scope asks. Escalate: P1 incidents, legal, refunds over threshold. Cite: numbers, policy quotes, SLAs.

### Beat 2

Write the exact phrases you want: 'Unknown — escalate to ops lead' beats silent guessing.

### Beat 3

Citations build trust with teammates who did not write the assistant.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“At Acme Ops, assistants only work when teammates trust them. This lesson locks that trust.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Stay model-agnostic: the same pattern applies whether you ship a Custom GPT, a Project, or a Gem.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done when you can apply this on a fictional Acme Ops workflow without guessing.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Guess if unsure and sound confident.

**After**

> If not in knowledge: say Unknown and propose escalate. When quoting SLA hours, cite Vendor_SLA_v3 section.
