# Module 3 — Files, knowledge & boundaries

**Slug:** `m03-files-knowledge-boundaries`  
**Course:** Custom GPTs & Team Assistants  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Curate knowledge packs with freshness rules and refuse/escalate/cite boundaries—so assistants stay useful without leaking or hallucinating policy.

## Why this module ships here

Files are not magic memory. Bad knowledge packs create confident wrong answers and privacy risk. Module 3 makes knowledge a product.
