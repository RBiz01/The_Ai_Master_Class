**Slug:** `m06-team-rollout-governance`

**Status:** Fully built

## Goal

Roll out assistants with clear sharing, ownership, adoption playbooks, and policy so expertise scales without shadow-IT chaos.

## Why this module ships here

A great assistant nobody trusts—or everyone forks—is wasted work. Module 6 ships the operating system for team use.
