# Lesson 6.1 — Sharing models: private, team, public

**Module:** Team rollout & governance  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4–8 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Pick the minimum sharing scope that matches risk and audience for each Acme Ops assistant.

## Teaching

### Beat 1

Private: drafting and eval. Team: trusted workflows. Broader/public: only scrubbed, low-risk helpers.

### Beat 2

Never share assistants that contain sensitive knowledge packs outside the need-to-know group.

### Beat 3

Model-agnostic: same privacy ladder applies across GPT sharing, Project members, and Gem sharing.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“At Acme Ops, assistants only work when teammates trust them. This lesson locks that trust.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Stay model-agnostic: the same pattern applies whether you ship a Custom GPT, a Project, or a Gem.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done when you can apply this on a fictional Acme Ops workflow without guessing.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Publish every draft assistant link in the company Slack.

**After**

> Vendor Triage: Acme Ops team only. Public: Acme Ops Glossary Helper with no internal SLAs.
