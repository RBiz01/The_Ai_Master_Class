# Lesson 6.4 — Policy, audit & sunset

**Module:** Team rollout & governance  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4–8 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

## Learning objective

State what is allowed, how you audit, and when to sunset or merge assistants.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Policy, audit & sunset).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *State what is allowed, how you audit, and when to sunset or merge assistants.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Beat 1

Policy: no secrets in knowledge, no write actions without HITL, team share requires owner.

### Beat 2

Audit: quarterly list of assistants, owners, last eval date, sharing scope.

### Beat 3

Sunset: merge duplicates, archive unused, announce retirement date.

**After**
