# Lesson 6.3 — Adoption playbooks & office hours

**Module:** Team rollout & governance  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4–8 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Plan a two-week rollout: announce, demo, office hours, feedback loop, and success metric.

## Lesson video

![Lesson video](assets/l03-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l03-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Beat 1

Adoption is a product launch, not a link drop.

### Beat 2

Week 1: announce + live demo. Week 2: office hours + collect golden-set misses.

### Beat 3

Metric example: % of standup briefs drafted via the assistant, not vanity open counts.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“At Acme Ops, assistants only work when teammates trust them. This lesson locks that trust.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Stay model-agnostic: the same pattern applies whether you ship a Custom GPT, a Project, or a Gem.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done when you can apply this on a fictional Acme Ops workflow without guessing.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> Drop a link: 'new GPT, enjoy'.

**After**

> Playbook: Slack announce, 15-min demo, Tue office hours, feedback form, target 60% standup use in 2 weeks.
