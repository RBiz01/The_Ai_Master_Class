**Module:** Anatomy of a custom assistant

## Learning objective

Name instructions, knowledge, actions, conversation starters, and sharing—and assign each Acme Ops content to the right surface.

3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: The five surfaces).

5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Name instructions, knowledge, actions, conversation starters, and sharing—and assign each Acme Ops content to the right surface.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Beat 1

Instructions = behavior contract. Knowledge = reference files with boundaries. Actions = optional tools. Starters = on-ramps. Sharing = who can use and edit.

### Beat 2

Model-agnostic: Custom GPTs, Claude Projects, Gemini Gems, and similar shells expose the same five ideas under different labels.

### Beat 3

Acme Ops mistake: stuffing policy into instructions AND uploading a conflicting PDF. Pick one source of truth per fact.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“At Acme Ops, assistants only work when teammates trust them. This lesson locks that trust.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Stay model-agnostic: the same pattern applies whether you ship a Custom GPT, a Project, or a Gem.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done when you can apply this on a fictional Acme Ops workflow without guessing.”
