# Lesson 2.1 — Role, mission & non-goals

**Module:** Instructions that stick  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4–8 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Open every assistant with a crisp role, a mission you can check, and explicit non-goals that prevent scope creep.

![Lesson video](assets/l01-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l01-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Beat 1

Role answers who the assistant is for Acme Ops. Mission answers what success looks like. Non-goals answer what it must refuse.

### Beat 2

Non-goals prevent 'helpful' expansion into legal advice, sending email, or inventing policy.

### Beat 3

Model-agnostic: put this block first in Custom GPT instructions, Project custom instructions, or Gem instructions.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“At Acme Ops, assistants only work when teammates trust them. This lesson locks that trust.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Stay model-agnostic: the same pattern applies whether you ship a Custom GPT, a Project, or a Gem.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done when you can apply this on a fictional Acme Ops workflow without guessing.”

## Worked micro-example

**Before**

> You are a helpful assistant for Acme Ops.

**After**

> Role: Acme Ops Vendor Triage coach for ops leads. Mission: rank vendor emails and draft replies for human send. Non-goals: do not send email, invent SLAs, or give legal advice.
