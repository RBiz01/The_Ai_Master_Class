## Teaching

### Beat 1

An output contract is the default skeleton: sections, length, labels, and empty fields when unknown.

### Beat 2

Conversation starters are UX: they teach teammates how to use the assistant without reading the instructions.

### Beat 3

Acme Ops Vendor Triage starters: Rank today's vendor emails · Draft a reply · Escalate with rationale.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)*  
“At Acme Ops, assistants only work when teammates trust them. This lesson locks that trust.”

*(Beat 2)*  
“Stay model-agnostic: the same pattern applies whether you ship a Custom GPT, a Project, or a Gem.”

*(Beat 3)*  
“You’re done when you can apply this on a fictional Acme Ops workflow without guessing.”

**After**
