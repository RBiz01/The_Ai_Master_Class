# Module 2 — Instructions that stick

**Slug:** `m02-instructions-that-stick`  
**Course:** Custom GPTs & Team Assistants  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Write assistant instructions as a durable behavior contract: mission, non-goals, hard rules, style, and output shape—without a 10,000-word novel.

## Why this module ships here

Shareable assistants live or die on instructions. Vague missions create vibe drift across teammates; conflicting rules create random refusals.
