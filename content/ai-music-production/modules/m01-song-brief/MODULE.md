# Module 1 — Song brief & taste lock

**Slug:** `m01-song-brief`  
**Course:** AI Music Production  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Lock a shippable song brief—genre, mood, reference, length, and taste locks—before you generate a single bar.

## Why this module ships first

Vague prompts waste credits. Module 1 installs a brief OS so Northstar Studio regenerations stay on-taste.
