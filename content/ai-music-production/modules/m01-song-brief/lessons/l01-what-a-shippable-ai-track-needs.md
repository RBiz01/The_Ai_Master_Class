## Teaching

### Shippable is more than a cool clip

At **Northstar Studio**, producer Riley ships AI-assisted tracks for client spots and catalog drops. A shippable track is not 'a generator spit something cool.' It has: (1) a locked brief, (2) a memorable hook, (3) clear structure (verse/chorus/bridge or equivalent), (4) mix notes that survive regeneration, and (5) release metadata + rights posture.

```text
Define a shippable AI track checklist for Northstar Studio:
- Brief fields (genre, mood, BPM range, length, vocal/instrumental)
- Hook test (one line a stranger can hum)
- Structure map (section times)
- Mix notes (loud/muddy/empty fixes)
- Release pack (title, credits, cover brief, platform description)
Do not invent artist names or paste copyrighted lyrics.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Shippable means brief + hook + structure + mix notes + release pack.”

*(Beat 2)* “Cool 20-second generator clips are sketches—not releases.”

### Worked micro-example

**Brief:** indie dream-pop, wistful night drive, ~90 BPM, 2:40, soft female vocal vibe.
**Hook test:** 'City lights blur like unfinished dreams.'
**Structure:** Intro 0:00–0:12 · V1 · Pre · Chorus · V2 · Chorus · Bridge · Final chorus · Outro.
