## Teaching

### Fail modes you can catch in 30 seconds

Northstar Studio QA flags: (1) **Vague**—'make it epic'; (2) **Clone**—'exactly like [famous song]'; (3) **Lyric sludge**—pasting full copyrighted lyrics; (4) **Length chaos**—no duration; (5) **Conflict**—'aggressive metal lullaby.' Rewrite each into brief-safe language.

```text
Rewrite these bad prompts into brief-safe versions:
1) make it epic
2) sound exactly like [Famous Hit]
3) use these full copyrighted lyrics: …
4) just generate something
5) heavy metal soft lullaby at once
Output: Failure | Fix
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Vague, clone, lyric dump, length chaos, conflict—name them to kill them.”

*(Beat 2)* “Your fix is always: brief fields + taste locks + original lyrics.”

### Worked micro-example

**Bad:** make it epic → **Fix:** cinematic indie rock, rising drums at 1:10, wide chorus, 2:30.
**Bad:** clone Famous Hit → **Fix:** 2010s stadium-pop energy, four-on-floor, bright synth stab—original melody.
