## Teaching

### Taste locks beat lucky takes

A taste lock is a short rule set that survives model randomness: **Always** (bright chorus lift, clean diction), **Never** (harsh metal screams, slurred mumbling, NSFW), **Prefer** (minor→major lift on chorus). Paste the locks into every generation and variation prompt.

```text
Create taste locks for Northstar Studio dream-pop brief:
ALWAYS: …
NEVER: …
PREFER: …
Keep locks under 12 lines. Model-agnostic. Classroom-safe.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Always / Never / Prefer—three buckets that travel with every prompt.”

*(Beat 2)* “Regeneration without locks is a lottery.”

### Worked micro-example

**ALWAYS:** soft consonants on verses; chorus opens with wider pads; end on resolved chord.
**NEVER:** screamed vocals; explicit content; sudden genre jumps mid-song.
**PREFER:** 2–3 word hook phrases; spare verses; lush choruses.
