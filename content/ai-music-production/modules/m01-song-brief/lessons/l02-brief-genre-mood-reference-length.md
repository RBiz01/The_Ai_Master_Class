**Module:** Song brief & taste lock

## Learning objective

Write a one-page song brief that locks genre, mood, reference vibe (not a clone), and target length.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: brief: genre, mood, reference, length).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Write a one-page song brief that locks genre, mood, reference vibe (not a clone), and target length.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Four locks that stop random generations

Northstar Studio's brief template: **Genre** (primary + 1 adjacent), **Mood** (3 adjectives max), **Reference vibe** (era/feel—never 'sound exactly like [famous artist]'), **Length** (target seconds + fade/end style). Add optional BPM range and vocal/instrumental decision.

```text
Fill this brief for Northstar Studio track 'Glass Horizon':
Genre: ____ (primary) + ____ (adjacent)
Mood: three adjectives
Reference vibe: era + instrumentation feel (no named living artist clone)
Length: mm:ss · ending style
BPM range · vocal/instrumental
Do-nots: no NSFW, no real artist names, no full copyrighted lyrics.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Genre + mood + reference vibe + length—four locks before generate.”

*(Beat 2)* “Reference the feel, not a clone request.”

### Worked micro-example

**Genre:** synthwave + soft indie
**Mood:** nostalgic, wide, hopeful
**Reference vibe:** late-80s neon highway feel, gated drums, airy pads
**Length:** 2:45 · soft fade
**BPM:** 100–108 · soft male vocal
