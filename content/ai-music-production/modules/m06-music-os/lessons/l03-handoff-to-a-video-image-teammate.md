## Teaching

### Handoff = audio + map + art brief + do-nots

Packet: final audio link/path, arrangement map with timestamps for cut points, cover/visual brief, mood adjectives, do-nots (no NSFW, no celeb likeness), and contact learner@example.com for questions. This course is music-only—handoff respects the separate generative-media lane.

```text
Write a one-page handoff for Glass Horizon to a video teammate:
Audio · Cut points · Visual brief · Do-nots · Contact
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Timestamps for cuts save your teammate's afternoon.”

*(Beat 2)* “Music course handoff ≠ doing their image/video job for them.”

### Worked micro-example

Cut on chorus hit 0:45 and 1:30. Visual: pink-violet night glass. Do-not: celebrity faces, NSFW.

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Handoff with audio, cut map, visual brief, and do-nots.
