# Lesson 6.4 — Ship one track this week

**Module:** A weekly music OS  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Commit to a weekly ship: one track through brief → generate → mix notes → release checklist.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: ship one track this week).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Commit to a weekly ship: one track through brief → generate → mix notes → release checklist.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Done is a date

Northstar weekly ship rule: pick one track by Monday batch, freeze brief by Tuesday, generate/iterate Wednesday, mix notes Thursday, release checklist Friday. Share privately with learner@example.com or publish if terms allow.

```text
Write a one-week ship plan for Glass Horizon with daily outcomes and a Friday definition of done (checklist complete + uploaded or internal archive).
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “One track. One week. Definition of done on Friday.”

*(Beat 2)* “Shipping beats infinite regeneration.”

### Worked micro-example

Fri DoD: checklist complete · WAV+MP3 archived · metadata sheet · cover brief sent · description pasted.

## Practice

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Calendar the ship. Friday needs a checked checklist, not another regen.
