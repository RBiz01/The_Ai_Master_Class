# Lesson 6.1 — Batch writing sessions

**Module:** A weekly music OS  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Design a 90-minute batch session that produces multiple briefs and hooks without premature generation.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: batch writing sessions).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Design a 90-minute batch session that produces multiple briefs and hooks without premature generation.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Write first, generate second

Northstar weekly batch (90 min): 20 min briefs ×3, 30 min hooks/lyrics, 20 min structure maps, 20 min pick one track to generate. Separating writing from generation protects taste locks.

```text
Build a 90-minute batch agenda for Northstar Studio with timers and outputs (3 briefs, 3 hooks, 1 chosen generate candidate).
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Batch briefs and hooks before you open the generator.”

*(Beat 2)* “Pick one candidate to generate—don't generate all three cold.”

### Worked micro-example

0:00–0:20 three briefs · 0:20–0:50 hooks · 0:50–1:10 maps · 1:10–1:30 choose + first-pass prompt

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Batch the writing; generate only the winner.
