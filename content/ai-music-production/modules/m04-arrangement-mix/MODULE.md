# Module 4 — Arrangement & mix notes

**Slug:** `m04-arrangement-mix`  
**Course:** AI Music Production  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Build arrangement maps models can follow, think in stems without real stems, write mix notes, and match references ethically.

## Why this module ships here

Generators need arrangement language. Module 4 gives mix vocabulary that transfers across tools.
