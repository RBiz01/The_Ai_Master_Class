## Teaching

### Reference the traits, not the identity

Legal/ethics posture at Northstar: describe **traits**—tempo feel, drum pattern family, vocal intimacy, stereo width—not 'make it indistinguishable from [hit].' Compare your take with a trait checklist, then adjust.

```text
Build a reference-trait checklist (no artist clone asks):
Tempo feel · Drum family · Vocal distance · Stereo width · Section contrast
Score Glass Horizon take vs checklist 1–5 each.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Traits yes. Clone requests no.”

*(Beat 2)* “Score your take against traits, then adjust.”

### Worked micro-example

Trait: intimate verse vocal (close mic feel) · Score 4
Trait: chorus width jump · Score 2 → widen pads, add delay throw

Match reference traits with a checklist—never ask for a clone.
