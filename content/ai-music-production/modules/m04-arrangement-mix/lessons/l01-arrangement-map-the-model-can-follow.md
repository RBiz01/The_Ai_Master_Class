# Lesson 4.1 — Arrangement map the model can follow

**Module:** Arrangement & mix notes  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Produce a timestamped arrangement map with energy levels the generator can follow.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: arrangement map the model can follow).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Produce a timestamped arrangement map with energy levels the generator can follow.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Energy curves beat vague 'build'

Northstar arrangement map: timestamp · section · instruments in · energy 1–5. Example: 0:45 Chorus · add snare + wider pads · energy 4. Paste the map into generation and variation prompts.

```text
Build an arrangement map for Glass Horizon (2:40) with columns:
Time | Section | Instruments | Energy 1–5 | Note
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Timestamps + energy numbers give the model a curve.”

*(Beat 2)* “Name instruments in/out per section.”

### Worked micro-example

1:55 Bridge · strip drums to kick+pad · energy 2 · vocal dry
2:10 Final chorus · full kit + harmony · energy 5

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Paste a timed energy map so arrangements don't flatline.
