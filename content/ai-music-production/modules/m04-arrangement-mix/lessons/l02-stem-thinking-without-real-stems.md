# Lesson 4.2 — Stem thinking without real stems

**Module:** Arrangement & mix notes  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Describe vocal, drums, bass, and music beds as virtual stems even when the tool only exports a stereo mix.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: stem thinking without real stems).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Describe vocal, drums, bass, and music beds as virtual stems even when the tool only exports a stereo mix.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Talk in stems so edits stay surgical

Even without true stems, prompt as if: **Vocal**, **Drums**, **Bass**, **Harmony/Pads**, **FX**. Ask for 'instrumental bed' or 'drums quieter in verse' language. Hand virtual-stem notes to mix and video teammates.

```text
Write virtual-stem notes for Glass Horizon:
Vocal: …
Drums: …
Bass: …
Pads: …
FX: …
Include one 'leave space for VO' note for ad cutdowns.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Name five virtual stems even on stereo exports.”

*(Beat 2)* “Stem language makes variation prompts surgical.”

### Worked micro-example

Drums: dry kick in verse; open hats only in chorus.
Pads: mono-ish in verse, stereo wide in chorus.

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Prompt and handoff in virtual stems—even without real stem exports.
