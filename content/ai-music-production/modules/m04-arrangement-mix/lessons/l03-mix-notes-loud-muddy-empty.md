## Teaching

### Three mix complaints, three fixes

**Too loud/harsh** → soften top end, reduce distortion, lower chorus vocal shout. **Muddy** → carve low-mids, simplify bass+kick overlap, thin verse pads. **Empty** → add counter-melody, widen chorus, lift percussion detail—without changing genre.

```text
Diagnose and fix:
Take A: harsh on earbuds
Take B: undefined bass vs kick
Take C: chorus feels hollow
Format: Problem | Mix note (1–2 lines)
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Loud, muddy, empty—name it, then prescribe.”

*(Beat 2)* “Mix notes should not rewrite the song's identity.”

### Worked micro-example

Muddy → 'High-pass pads at ~180Hz; sidechain bass lightly to kick; keep vocal forward.'
