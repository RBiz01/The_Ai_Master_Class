## Teaching

### Metadata is part of the song

Release pack fields: Title, Primary artist (Northstar Studio), Featured (if any), Writers, Producers, Year, Genre tags, ISRC placeholder, explicit flag (clean), Cover-art brief (mood, palette, no real celeb likeness).

```text
Fill a metadata sheet for Glass Horizon + a 5-bullet cover-art brief for the image teammate.
Palette hint: pink→violet (#ec4899 → #8b5cf6). No NSFW. No real celebrity faces.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Title, credits, genre tags, explicit flag, cover brief.”

*(Beat 2)* “Cover brief talks mood and palette—not 'draw this famous person.'”

### Worked micro-example

Title: Glass Horizon · Artist: Northstar Studio · Explicit: No
Cover: night highway glass reflections, pink-violet glow, no text-heavy mess, square 3000px.
