## Teaching

### Describe the song, don't bait search with brands

Avoid 'sounds like [Famous Band]' in public descriptions. Avoid medical/financial claims in lyric stories. Keep classroom-safe. Include honest AI-assisted note where the platform expects transparency.

```text
Rewrite this unsafe blurb:
'Glass Horizon sounds exactly like [Famous Band] — guaranteed to go viral and cure your sadness.'
Make it platform-safe for Northstar Studio.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “No 'sounds exactly like' public bait. No cure claims.”

*(Beat 2)* “Describe mood, genre, and use-case honestly.”

### Worked micro-example

'Glass Horizon is a wistful dream-pop single from Northstar Studio—night-drive mood, AI-assisted production with human lyric edits. Clean version.'

## Practice

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Public copy: mood + genre + honest process—no clone bait or miracle claims.
