# Lesson 5.4 — A one-track release checklist

**Module:** Rights, credits, release  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Run a final one-track release checklist covering audio, rights, metadata, art, and platform copy.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: a one-track release checklist).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Run a final one-track release checklist covering audio, rights, metadata, art, and platform copy.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### One checklist, zero oops

Northstar one-track gate: audio loudness OK, mix notes resolved, authorship blurb, metadata sheet, cover art, platform description, AI-tool terms reviewed, learner contact learner@example.com on internal sheet only.

```text
Produce a printable one-track release checklist for Glass Horizon with 10 checkbox items covering audio, rights, metadata, art, and copy.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Audio, rights, metadata, art, copy—five buckets.”

*(Beat 2)* “If any box is open, don't upload yet.”

### Worked micro-example

Final WAV + MP3 · Authorship blurb · Cover 3000×3000 · Platform-safe description · Terms reviewed

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.
