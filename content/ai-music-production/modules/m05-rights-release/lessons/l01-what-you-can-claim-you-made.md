**Module:** Rights, credits, release  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive

## Learning objective

Write honest authorship claims for AI-assisted music without overstating or hiding AI use.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: what you can claim you made).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Write honest authorship claims for AI-assisted music without overstating or hiding AI use.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Honest credit beats marketing fog

Northstar Studio default: 'Written & produced by Northstar Studio with AI-assisted generation and human arrangement/lyric edits.' Don't claim 100% human performance if vocals are model-generated. Don't claim you own a famous melody you didn't write. Follow your tool's commercial terms (model-agnostic reminder).

```text
Draft 3 authorship blurbs for Glass Horizon:
1) Catalog single
2) Client commercial bed
3) Internal demo
Each must disclose AI assistance honestly without legal overclaim.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Disclose AI assistance. Don't claim famous melodies.”

*(Beat 2)* “Check your generator's commercial terms before monetizing.”

### Worked micro-example

'Glass Horizon — Northstar Studio. Lyrics & arrangement by Riley Chen. Vocal and instrumental beds AI-assisted; human mix notes and final edit.'

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Claim your creative work; disclose AI assistance; never claim others' melodies.
