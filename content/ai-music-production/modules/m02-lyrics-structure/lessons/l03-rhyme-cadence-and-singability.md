## Teaching

### Singability is a constraint, not a vibe

Count stressed syllables per line. Prefer open vowels on long notes. Avoid stacking consonants ('strict script scraps'). Perfect rhyme every line is optional—slant rhyme + cadence often sings better.

```text
Audit this Northstar draft for singability:
'Strict scripts scrap the streetlight's stubborn shine'
Rewrite for open vowels, ~8–10 syllables, soft consonants, slant rhyme OK.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Count stresses. Prefer open vowels on sustained notes.”

*(Beat 2)* “Slant rhyme + clean cadence beats forced perfect rhyme.”

### Worked micro-example

**Before:** Strict scripts scrap the streetlight's stubborn shine
**After:** Soft light leans across the quiet line
