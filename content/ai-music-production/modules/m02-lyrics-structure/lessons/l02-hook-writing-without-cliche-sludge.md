## Teaching

### Specific beats generic

Cliché sludge: 'chase the dream,' 'heart of gold,' 'rise up tonight.' Northstar rule: **concrete image + emotion + short syllables**. Test: can a stranger hum it after one listen?

```text
Rewrite these cliché hooks into specific, singable lines for a wistful night-drive dream-pop track:
- chase the dream
- heart of gold
- rise up tonight
Constraints: ≤10 words each, original, classroom-safe, no real artist quotes.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Concrete image + emotion + short syllables.”

*(Beat 2)* “If a stranger can't hum it, it's not a hook yet.”

### Worked micro-example

**Cliché:** chase the dream → **Hook:** 'We count the exits we never take.'
**Cliché:** rise up tonight → **Hook:** 'Neon lifts us one floor higher.'

## Takeaway

Kill clichés with concrete images and a hum test.
