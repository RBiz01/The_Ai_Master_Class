## Teaching

### Every section needs a job

At Northstar Studio: **Verse** advances story/detail, **Pre** raises tension, **Chorus** states the thesis hook, **Bridge** offers a new angle or contrast, **Outro** resolves. Tell the model section jobs + approximate lengths.

```text
Write a structure map for 'Glass Horizon' (2:40):
Section | Job | ~seconds | Lyric intent (1 line)
Include Intro, V1, Pre, Chorus, V2, Chorus, Bridge, Final Chorus, Outro.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Verse tells, chorus declares, bridge contrasts.”

*(Beat 2)* “Give the model jobs and times, not 'write a song.'”

### Worked micro-example

Chorus job: declare the title hook in 1–2 lines.
Bridge job: switch to second-person confession, then return.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Assign each section a job and a time budget before writing lines.
