## Teaching

### Three passes, one goal

Northstar Studio loop: **Pass A** clarify meaning, **Pass B** upgrade images, **Pass C** fix singability. Never paste full copyrighted lyrics into the model. Original lines only; short public-domain fragments OK if labeled.

```text
Run a 3-pass rewrite on this original Northstar draft chorus:
'Lights go by / we forget why / still we try'
Pass A clarity · Pass B image · Pass C singability. Keep title hook 'Glass Horizon' optional.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Clarity, then image, then singability—don't mix passes.”

*(Beat 2)* “Original lyrics only. No full copyrighted pastes.”

### Worked micro-example

A: We watch the lights and lose the reason, but we keep going.
B: Exit signs smear gold across the glass horizon.
C: Exit signs smear gold / across our glass horizon

## Practice

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Loop clarity → image → singability on original lines only.
