# Module 2 — Lyrics & structure

## Goal

Write verse, chorus, and bridge that land—hooks without cliché sludge, singable rhyme and cadence, and rewrite loops.

## Why this module ships here

Generators amplify weak lyrics. Module 2 installs structure and rewrite habits before generation.
