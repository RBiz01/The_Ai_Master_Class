## Teaching

### Paste the brief, don't summarize it away

Model-agnostic pattern: paste Genre, Mood, Reference vibe, Length, BPM, Vocal decision, Structure map, Taste locks, and original lyrics (or instrumental note). Ask for 2–3 candidates scored against the brief.

```text
Using ONLY this brief, generate 2 candidate track descriptions + prompt packs.
<brief>…paste Northstar Glass Horizon brief…</brief>
Score each: Brief match 1–5 · Hook strength 1–5 · Structure clarity 1–5.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “First pass = brief verbatim + locks + original lyrics.”

*(Beat 2)* “Score candidates against the brief, not vibes alone.”

### Worked micro-example

Candidate A score: Brief 5 · Hook 4 · Structure 5 → keep.
Candidate B score: Brief 2 (tempo drift) → discard or re-lock BPM.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

First-pass prompts paste the whole brief and score the outputs.
