## Teaching

### Edit the take; regenerate the miss

**Regenerate** when genre, mood, or hook identity miss the brief. **Edit / extend / vary** when the spine is right but arrangement, length, or mix notes need work. Don't regenerate just because you're impatient.

```text
Classify each Northstar take:
A) Perfect hook, muddy low end
B) Wrong genre family
C) Good verse, weak chorus lift
Output: Regenerate or Edit + why (1 line).
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Spine wrong → regenerate. Spine right → edit/vary.”

*(Beat 2)* “Muddy mix is usually an edit note, not a full regen.”

### Worked micro-example

A → Edit (mix notes).
B → Regenerate (genre miss).
C → Vary chorus arrangement / rewrite hook lyric.

## Takeaway

Regenerate for identity misses; edit for arrangement and mix.
