## Teaching

### Use-case picks the vocal lane

Northstar Studio lanes: **Lead vocal** for singles; **Instrumental + optional hum** for ads; **Bed/underscore** for video teammates (no competing vocal). State gender-neutral 'vocal vibe' if needed, or 'instrumental only—leave space for VO.'

```text
For each use-case pick vocal lane + one prompt line:
1) Spotify single
2) 30s product spot with VO
3) YouTube vertical with on-screen text
Northstar Studio / classroom-safe.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Single → lead vocal. Spot with VO → instrumental bed.”

*(Beat 2)* “Say 'leave space for VO' when video owns the words.”

### Worked micro-example

Spot + VO: instrumental dream-pop bed, no lead vocal, soft sidechain under imagined VO.
