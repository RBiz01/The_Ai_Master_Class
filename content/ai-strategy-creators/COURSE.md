## Who this is for

Creators, freelancers, and founders who want AI as **leverage**—not another shiny tab. You ship offers, content, and workflows; you need strategy, packaging, and operating cadence.

No engineering degree required. Patterns work across major assistants and no-code tools.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Map niche pain to **AI leverage** and score opportunities.
2. Productize personal hacks into **workflows and packages**.
3. **Position and price** against DIY ChatGPT objections with proof stacks.
4. Build **content systems** that compound without sounding automated.
5. Run a **team of one** with agents, contractors, and cadence.
6. Choose **moats, ethics controls, and long-game bets** with kill criteria.

## Prerequisites

- An offer or niche you want to grow (we use fictional **Northstar Studio** / **Acme Ops**)
- Access to any modern LLM chat interface
