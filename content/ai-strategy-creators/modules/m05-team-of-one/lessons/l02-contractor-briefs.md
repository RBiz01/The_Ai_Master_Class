## Teaching

### Brief that works

Goal, context, constraints, format, examples, deadline, escalation, definition of done.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Vague briefs create expensive ping-pong.
*(Beat 2)* Exemplars teach faster than adjectives.
*(Beat 3)* Definition of done prevents almost-done.
