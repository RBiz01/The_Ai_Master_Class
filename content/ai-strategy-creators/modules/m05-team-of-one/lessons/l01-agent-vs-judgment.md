## Teaching

### Split the work

**Agents/automation:** draft, retrieve, format, remind.
**Humans:** claims, pricing, client taste, ethics calls, final send.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Agents draft; humans decide.
*(Beat 2)* Judgment gates are product features.
*(Beat 3)* Automating judgment is how brands get burned.
