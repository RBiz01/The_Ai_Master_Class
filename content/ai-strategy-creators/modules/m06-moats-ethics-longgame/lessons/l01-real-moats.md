## Teaching

### Moats beyond 'we use GPT'

Data loops, distribution, brand taste, workflow IP, relationships, switching costs, compliance trust.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Models commoditize; your loop might not.
*(Beat 2)* Name the moat in one sentence.
*(Beat 3)* If the moat is only the model, you do not have one.
