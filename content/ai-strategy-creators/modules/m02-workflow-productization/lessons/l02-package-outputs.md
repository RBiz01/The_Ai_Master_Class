## Teaching

### Packaging that sells

Sell outcomes and artifacts, not 'we use AI.' Define scope, revisions, and what is human-reviewed.

### Offer skeleton

- Deliverable name
- Included / not included
- Turnaround
- Revision policy
- Proof of process

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Buyers buy packages, not prompts.
*(Beat 2)* Scope prevents AI-speed from becoming scope creep.
*(Beat 3)* Human review is a feature you can price.
