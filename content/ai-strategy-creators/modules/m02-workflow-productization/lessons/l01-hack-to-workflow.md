## Teaching

### From hack to named workflow

A ChatGPT habit becomes a product when it has: name, trigger, inputs, steps, output, QA gate, owner.

### Northstar example

'Brief Doctor' — trigger: new inquiry; inputs: call notes; steps: retrieve similar briefs, draft, human edit; output: client-ready brief v1.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Name it or it stays a personal trick.
*(Beat 2)* Triggers and QA gates make it trainable.
*(Beat 3)* If only you can run it, it is not productized.
