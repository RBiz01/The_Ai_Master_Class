## Teaching

### SOPs for contractors

Write steps a stranger can run: tools, logins (sandbox), examples of good/bad output, escalation rules.

### Acme Ops test

Hand the SOP to a contractor for one ticket type. If they ping you more than twice, the SOP failed — not the contractor.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* SOPs are how leverage scales past your calendar.
*(Beat 2)* Good/bad exemplars beat abstract rules.
*(Beat 3)* Escalation paths protect quality.
