## Teaching

### Pain before tools

At **Northstar Studio**, list niche pains first: slow proposals, inconsistent briefs, buried past work. Only then ask where AI removes friction.

### Leverage hypothesises

| Pain | AI leverage hypothesis |
|------|------------------------|
| Proposals take 6 hours | Draft in 40 min with human edit gate |
| Past briefs unfindable | Similarity search over archive |

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Map pain, then leverage — never the reverse.
*(Beat 2)* A hypothesis names who, task, and time saved.
*(Beat 3)* Shiny tools without pain maps become shelfware.
