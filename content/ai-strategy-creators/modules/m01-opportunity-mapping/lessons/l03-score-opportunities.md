## Teaching

### Simple scorecard

Score 1-5 on: leverage (time/money), proof ease, distribution fit, ethics/risk, moat potential.
Priority = leverage x proof ease (start simple).

### Kill early

Low proof + high hype = park it. High leverage + easy proof = this week's experiment.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Scorecards beat vibes in a founder panic.
*(Beat 2)* Multiply leverage by proof ease.
*(Beat 3)* Park shiny low-proof bets without guilt.
