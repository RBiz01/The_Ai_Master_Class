## Teaching

### Three paths

- **Build** when the workflow is your secret sauce and data is yours.
- **Buy** when a vendor already solves 80% and switching costs are low.
- **Wrap** when you productize a model API behind your brand, SOP, and QA.

### Acme Ops example

Triage classifier: wrap a no-code tool with Acme Ops taxonomy and human override — do not rebuild ML.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Build/buy/wrap is a portfolio decision.
*(Beat 2)* Wrapping is how solo founders ship fast with taste.
*(Beat 3)* Revisit the choice when volume or risk changes.
