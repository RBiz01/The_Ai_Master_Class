## Teaching

### The ChatGPT objection

Buyers say they can DIY. Your positioning must answer: speed with taste, risk reduction, distribution, accountability, and proven SOPs.

### Positioning line

Northstar does not sell 'AI writing.' It sells launch briefs with retrieval from three years of wins plus a producer QA gate.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* DIY is free and unfinished.
*(Beat 2)* Sell the system and the accountability.
*(Beat 3)* Specific beats 'powered by AI.'
