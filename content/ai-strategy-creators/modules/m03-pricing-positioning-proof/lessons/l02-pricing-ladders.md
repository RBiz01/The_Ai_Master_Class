## Teaching

### Three rungs

1. **Self-serve / light** — templates + light AI assist
2. **Core** — done-with-you workflow
3. **Premium** — done-for-you with SLA and human QA

Price the judgment and distribution, not the tokens.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Ladders catch different willingness to pay.
*(Beat 2)* Premium sells risk removal.
*(Beat 3)* Token cost is not your price floor alone.
