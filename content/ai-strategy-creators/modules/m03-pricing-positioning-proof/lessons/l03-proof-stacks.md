## Teaching

### Proof stack layers

Demo -> mini case study -> metric -> guarantee/risk reversal -> referral.

Use fictional-safe classroom examples here; in real life, collect permissioned metrics.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* One proof is fragile; stacks convert.
*(Beat 2)* Show the workflow, not only the output.
*(Beat 3)* Guarantees must match error cost.
