## Teaching

### Taste locks

Ban generic AI tells. Keep a voice card: words we use, words we never use, claim rules, citation rules.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Repurpose with a human edit gate.
*(Beat 2)* Voice cards prevent automated mush.
*(Beat 3)* Claims need sources — especially with AI drafts.
