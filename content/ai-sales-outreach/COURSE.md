# AI for Sales & Outreach

**Course ID:** `ai-sales-outreach`  
**Tagline:** Research, sequences, and CRM-safe messaging that still sound human  
**Level:** Beginner · **Duration:** 6 hours · **Lessons:** 24  
**Pacing:** ~1 hour per curriculum module × 6 modules  
**Gradient:** `#3b82f6` → `#f59e0b`  
**Product:** The Ai Master Class

---

## Who this is for

SDRs, AEs, and founders who sell—and already dabble with AI drafts that sound robotic, invent prospect facts, or never make it into the CRM cleanly.

No coding required. Patterns work across major assistants. Scenarios use fictional **Northstar Studio** (seller) and **Acme Ops** (prospect) only.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Run **AI-assisted prospect research** that stays sourced and CRM-safe.
2. Write **outreach that doesn’t sound robotic**—signal-based, channel-fit, human.
3. Turn **call notes into CRM fields** and SDR→AE handoffs without inventing data.
4. Design **sequences and follow-ups** with value, stop rules, and reply branches.
5. Handle **objections, proposals, and decks** with honest pricing language.
6. Apply **brand, compliance, and automation guardrails** your team will actually follow.

## Prerequisites

- Access to any modern LLM chat interface
- A CRM you can practice field-mapping against (we’ll use fictional fields)
- Optional: your real sequence tool (habits transfer)
