## Learning objective

## Teaching

### Quality checklist before outreach

1. **Sourced** — every claim has a quote  
2. **Specific** — named pain, not “efficiency”  
3. **CRM-safe** — no invented people/budget  
4. **Outreach-ready** — one clear angle for the first email

Score 0–2 each. Ship only at 6+.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Bad research makes polished spam. Check the brief before you draft.”
