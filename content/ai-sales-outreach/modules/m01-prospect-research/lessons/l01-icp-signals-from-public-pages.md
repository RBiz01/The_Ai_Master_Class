## Teaching

### ICP signals only from what you paste

At **Northstar Studio**, SDR Jordan researches fictional prospect **Acme Ops**. Rule one: the model may only use text you paste (careers page, product page, press stub). No invented Series B, no fake VP names.

```text
Use ONLY the <source> text. List ICP signals as:
Signal | Evidence quote | Fit (High/Med/Low)
If missing, write Not in source.
<source>…paste public page…</source>
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Research is not creative writing. Starve invention with sources.”

*(Beat 2)* “Acme Ops careers mentioning ‘ops tooling pain’ is a signal. ‘They probably hate their CRM’ is fan fiction.”

### Worked micro-example

**Paste:** Acme Ops careers blurb about scaling vendor onboarding.  
**Signal:** Scaling ops / vendor onboarding · quote · High fit for Northstar’s ops enablement pitch.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Paste public sources. Quote every signal. No invented titles.
