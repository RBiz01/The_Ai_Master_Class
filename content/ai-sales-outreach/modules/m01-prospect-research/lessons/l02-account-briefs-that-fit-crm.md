# Lesson 1.2 — Account briefs that fit the CRM

**Module:** AI-assisted prospect research  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Compress research into a CRM-ready brief: company, pain hypothesis, proof, next ask.

## Lesson video

![Lesson video](assets/l02-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l02-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### CRM-ready briefs

Compress to fields your CRM will actually hold:

| Field | Example (Acme Ops) |
|-------|--------------------|
| Account | Acme Ops |
| Pain hypothesis | Vendor onboarding slow at scale |
| Proof | Careers + product page quotes |
| Likely buyer | Ops lead (title UNKNOWN if not sourced) |
| Next ask | 15-min fit call |

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If it doesn’t fit a CRM field, it’s a novel—not a brief.”

*(Beat 2)* “UNKNOWN beats a confident wrong title.”

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Brief = fields. UNKNOWN when unsourced.
