## Teaching

### Business triggers only

Good triggers: hiring surge on careers, product launch post, ops incident postmortem (public), new office, new market.

Bad triggers: kids’ sports, vacation photos, medical hints, anything that feels like stalking.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Personalization is not surveillance. Stay on the business stage.”

*(Beat 2)* “Northstar Studio SDRs win on relevance, not creepiness.”

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Business triggers only. Personal life is off-limits.
