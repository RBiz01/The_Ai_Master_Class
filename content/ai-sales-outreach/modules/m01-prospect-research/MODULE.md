# Module 1 — AI-assisted prospect research

**Slug:** `m01-prospect-research`  
**Course:** AI for Sales & Outreach  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Turn public signals into CRM-safe account briefs: ICP fit, trigger events, and a research quality checklist—without inventing facts about prospects.

## Why this module ships first

SDRs drown in tabs. Module 1 installs a research OS that feeds outreach and CRM without hallucinated titles or fake funding rounds.
