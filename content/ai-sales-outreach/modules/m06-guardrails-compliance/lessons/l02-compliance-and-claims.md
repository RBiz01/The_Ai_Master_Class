## Learning objective

Flag risky claims (guarantees, fake logos, CAN-SPAM tells) and rewrite safely.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Compliance and claims).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Flag risky claims (guarantees, fake logos, CAN-SPAM tells) and rewrite safely.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Claims & compliance

Flag: guarantees, medical/financial promises, harvested emails without consent framing, misleading subject lines, fake case studies.

Rewrite to accurate, permission-aware, CRM-logged outreach.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If Legal wouldn’t put it on the website, don’t put it in the sequence.”

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

No guarantees, no fake logos, no spam tells.
