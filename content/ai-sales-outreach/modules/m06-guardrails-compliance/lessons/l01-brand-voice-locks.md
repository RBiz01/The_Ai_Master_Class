## Learning objective

Encode Northstar Studio voice: warmth, directness, banned phrases, and do-nots.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Brand voice locks).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Encode Northstar Studio voice: warmth, directness, banned phrases, and do-nots.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.
