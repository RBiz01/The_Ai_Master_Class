## Beat sheet

1. **Cold open** — show the vague ask (30s).  
2. **Lock decisions** — sources, fields, channel, do-nots (90s).  
3. **Run the prompt** — narrate output shape (120s).  
4. **Score it** — checklist / CRM-safe pass (60s).  
5. **Ship** — what goes to CRM vs send button (45s).
