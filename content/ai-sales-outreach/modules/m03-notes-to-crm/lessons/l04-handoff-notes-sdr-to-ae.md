# Lesson 3.4 — Handoff notes: SDR to AE

**Module:** Call & meeting notes to CRM  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Write a handoff brief Acme Ops AE can run without pinging the SDR twice.

## What to do

1. Cue up the **Lesson video** and watch it once before you dig into the text.
2. Read **Teaching** carefully — especially the worked example you can reuse Monday.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Handoff notes: SDR to AE).
4. Apply the idea once in your words (sticky note or note app is fine).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Write a handoff brief Acme Ops AE can run without pinging the SDR twice.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.
