# Lesson 3.2 — CRM fields without invention

**Module:** Call & meeting notes to CRM  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Map notes to CRM fields; mark UNKNOWN—never invent budget, title, or timeline.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: CRM fields without invention).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Map notes to CRM fields; mark UNKNOWN—never invent budget, title, or timeline.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### CRM without invention

```text
Map to fields: Stage, Pain, Impact, Next step, Champion, Budget, Timeline.
If a field wasn’t said, write UNKNOWN — do not invent.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Invented budget is how forecasts lie. Prefer UNKNOWN.”

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Map fields. UNKNOWN beats fiction.
