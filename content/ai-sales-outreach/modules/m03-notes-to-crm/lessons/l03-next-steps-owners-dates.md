# Lesson 3.3 — Next steps with owners and dates

**Module:** Call & meeting notes to CRM  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Extract next steps as Owner · Task · Due from the call—no ghost assignees.

![Lesson video](assets/l03-video.mp4)

## Teaching

### Owner · Task · Due

Extract actions only if spoken. Missing owner/date → UNKNOWN. Never assign “Acme Ops team” as a fake person.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Ghost owners create ghost work.”

## Practice

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Owner · Task · Due. No ghost assignees.
