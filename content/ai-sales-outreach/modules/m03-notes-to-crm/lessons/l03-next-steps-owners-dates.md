# Lesson 3.3 — Next steps with owners and dates

**Module:** Call & meeting notes to CRM  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Extract next steps as Owner · Task · Due from the call—no ghost assignees.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once; pause only if a beat truly clicks for your role.
2. Work through **Teaching** and the worked example until you could explain it out loud.
3. Open `l03-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Next steps with owners and dates).
4. Jot one reusable line in your own words — something you’d actually use at work.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Extract next steps as Owner · Task · Due from the call—no ghost assignees.* and `l03-interactive.html` shows success.
