# Module 3 — Call & meeting notes to CRM

**Slug:** `m03-notes-to-crm`  
**Course:** AI for Sales & Outreach  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Turn messy call notes into structured CRM updates: fields without invention, next steps with owners, and clean SDR→AE handoffs.

## Why this module ships here

Deals die in unread notes. Module 3 makes AI the post-call operator—CRM-safe and handoff-ready.

## Success criteria for the learner

- Can structure call notes into Pain / Impact / Next step  
- Can fill CRM fields without inventing titles or budget  
- Can extract Owner · Task · Due with UNKNOWN when missing  
- Can write an SDR→AE handoff a stranger can run
