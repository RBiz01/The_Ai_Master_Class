# Module 5 — Objections, proposals & decks

**Slug:** `m05-objections-proposals`  
**Course:** AI for Sales & Outreach  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

## Goal

Map objections with AI, ship one-page proposals, build deck talk tracks, and keep pricing language honest.

## Why this module ships here

AI that overpromises loses deals. Module 5 helps you answer objections and package offers without fiction.
