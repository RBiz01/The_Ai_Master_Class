## Teaching

### Honest pricing language

State options and what’s included. Never invent discounts, “guaranteed ROI,” or logos you didn’t earn.

```text
If price wasn’t approved in the brief, say: “I’ll confirm options with my lead—here’s the range we typically use.”
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Overpromise is a refund with interest.”

## Takeaway

Options yes. Invented discounts no.
