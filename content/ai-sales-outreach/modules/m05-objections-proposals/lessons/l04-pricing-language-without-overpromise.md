# Lesson 5.4 — Pricing language without overpromise

**Module:** Objections, proposals & decks  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Explain price, options, and next steps—never invent discounts or guarantees.

![Lesson video](assets/l04-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l04-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Honest pricing language

State options and what’s included. Never invent discounts, “guaranteed ROI,” or logos you didn’t earn.

```text
If price wasn’t approved in the brief, say: “I’ll confirm options with my lead—here’s the range we typically use.”
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Overpromise is a refund with interest.”

## Takeaway

Options yes. Invented discounts no.
