## Teaching

### Objection map

For each objection: **Clarify → Reframe → Proof → Ask.**

Example: “Too expensive” → clarify compared-to-what → reframe cost of status quo → proof from similar ops team (fictional case) → ask which option to compare.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Don’t argue the first sentence. Clarify it.”

Clarify · Reframe · Proof · Ask.
