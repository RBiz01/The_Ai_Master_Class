# Lesson 5.1 — Objection maps with AI

**Module:** Objections, proposals & decks  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Turn common Acme Ops objections into Clarify → Reframe → Proof → Ask.

![Lesson video](assets/l01-video.mp4)

## Teaching

### Objection map

For each objection: **Clarify → Reframe → Proof → Ask.**

Example: “Too expensive” → clarify compared-to-what → reframe cost of status quo → proof from similar ops team (fictional case) → ask which option to compare.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Don’t argue the first sentence. Clarify it.”

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Clarify · Reframe · Proof · Ask.
