# Lesson 5.3 — Deck talk tracks

**Module:** Objections, proposals & decks  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Write slide-by-slide talk tracks a Northstar AE can say out loud in under 12 minutes.

## Lesson video

![Lesson video](assets/l03-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l03-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Talk tracks

Per slide: **Say this (≤25 sec) · Pause for · Don’t say.**  
Keep total spoken path under ~12 minutes for a Northstar demo deck.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Slides aren’t a script dump. Talk tracks are.”

## Practice

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Per slide: say · pause · don’t say.
