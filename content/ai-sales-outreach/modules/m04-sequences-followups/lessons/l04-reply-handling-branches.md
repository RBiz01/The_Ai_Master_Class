# Lesson 4.4 — Reply handling branches

**Module:** Sequences & smart follow-ups  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Draft reply branches for busy, not-now, send-info, and book-time—CRM notes included.

![Lesson video](assets/l04-video.mp4)

## Teaching

### Reply branches

| Reply | Move |
|-------|------|
| Busy | Offer time window + async option |
| Not now | Ask when + park in CRM |
| Send info | One-pager + one question |
| Interested | Two slots + calendar link |

Log the branch in CRM.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Replies are forks, not victories. Branch on purpose.”

## Practice

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Busy · not now · info · book. Log the branch.
