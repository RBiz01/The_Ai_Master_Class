## Teaching

### Breakup & re-engage

Breakup: permission to close the loop + soft door.  
Re-engage later: only with a **new** Acme Ops trigger (hiring, launch)—not guilt.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Graceful exits protect the brand. Desperate bumps burn it.”
