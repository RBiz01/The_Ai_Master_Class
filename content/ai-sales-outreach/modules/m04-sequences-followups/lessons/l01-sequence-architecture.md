# Lesson 4.1 — Sequence architecture that respects humans

**Module:** Sequences & smart follow-ups  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Design a Northstar Studio sequence: goal, touches, channels, stop rules.

## Lesson video

![Lesson video](assets/l01-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l01-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Sequence architecture

Define: **Goal · Ideal reply · Touches (5–7) · Channels · Stop rules** (booked, hard no, unsubscribe, 3 no-replies after breakup).

Each touch has a purpose—not “more of the same.”

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “A sequence without stop rules is spam with a calendar.”

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Goal, touches, channels, stop rules.
