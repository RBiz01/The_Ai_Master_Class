## Teaching

### Sequence architecture

Define: **Goal · Ideal reply · Touches (5–7) · Channels · Stop rules** (booked, hard no, unsubscribe, 3 no-replies after breakup).

Each touch has a purpose—not “more of the same.”

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “A sequence without stop rules is spam with a calendar.”
