# Lesson 4.2 — Follow-up cadence and value

**Module:** Sequences & smart follow-ups  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Every bump adds a micro-asset (insight, question, resource)—never ‘just checking in.’

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Follow-up cadence and value).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Every bump adds a micro-asset (insight, question, resource)—never ‘just checking in.’* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Value every bump

Banned: “Just checking in,” “Circling back,” “Bump.”  
Required: micro-insight, relevant question, tiny resource, or new public trigger.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If the bump adds nothing, don’t send it.”

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Every follow-up adds value—or don’t send.
