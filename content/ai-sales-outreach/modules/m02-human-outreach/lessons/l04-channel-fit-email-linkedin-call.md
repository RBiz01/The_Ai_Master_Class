## Teaching

### Channel kits

| Channel | Length | Shape |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Email | 80–120 words | Context → signal → ask |
| LinkedIn | 2–5 lines | Signal → question |
| Call opener | 20 seconds | Name · reason · permission |

Same idea, different costume.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Don’t paste the email into LinkedIn. Respect the channel.”
