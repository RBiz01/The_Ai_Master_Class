# Lesson 2.1 — Kill the AI sales accent

**Module:** Outreach that doesn’t sound robotic  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Strip hedges, fake urgency, and keynote fluff from Northstar Studio outreach drafts.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Kill the AI sales accent).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Strip hedges, fake urgency, and keynote fluff from Northstar Studio outreach drafts.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Kill the AI sales accent

Tells: “I hope this email finds you well,” “leverage synergies,” fake urgency (“quick question”), three symmetrical fluff bullets, inventing mutual connections.

**Rewrite add-on:** “Plain Northstar Studio voice. Short sentences. No hedges. No fake urgency. One ask.”

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “If it sounds like a template farm, it is.”

*(Beat 2)* “Humans skim. Earn the next line.”

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Plain sentences. One ask. No synergy.
