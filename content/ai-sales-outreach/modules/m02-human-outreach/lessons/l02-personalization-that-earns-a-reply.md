## Teaching

### One real signal

Personalization that earns a reply cites **one** concrete Acme Ops signal and connects it to a relevant Northstar outcome—then asks one question.

Fake: “Loved your recent thought leadership.”  
Real: “Saw Acme Ops hiring three vendor ops roles—curious how onboarding handoffs work today?”

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “One true detail beats five adjectives.”
