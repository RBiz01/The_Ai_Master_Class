# Lesson 2.2 — Personalization that earns a reply

**Module:** Outreach that doesn’t sound robotic  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Use one concrete Acme Ops signal—never fake compliments or invented mutuals.

![Lesson video](assets/l02-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l02-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### One real signal

Personalization that earns a reply cites **one** concrete Acme Ops signal and connects it to a relevant Northstar outcome—then asks one question.

Fake: “Loved your recent thought leadership.”  
Real: “Saw Acme Ops hiring three vendor ops roles—curious how onboarding handoffs work today?”

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “One true detail beats five adjectives.”

## Takeaway

One signal. One connection. One ask.
