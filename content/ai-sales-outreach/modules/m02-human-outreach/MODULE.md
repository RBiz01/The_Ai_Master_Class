# Module 2 — Outreach that doesn’t sound robotic

**Slug:** `m02-human-outreach`  
**Course:** AI for Sales & Outreach  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

## Goal

Draft cold and warm outreach that kills the AI sales accent, personalizes on real signals, and fits email, LinkedIn, or call openers.

## Why this module ships here

Generic AI emails get deleted. Module 2 makes every line sound like a sharp Northstar Studio human—not a sequence bot.
