## Talk-over (narration)

*(0:00–0:25)*  
Welcome to Module 2 of **Prompt Engineering Mastery** on The Ai Master Class. Module 1 made prompts clear. This hour makes them **controllable**—roles that change decisions, constraints that don’t fight each other, and formats that parse.

*(0:25–0:55)*  
At fictional Acme Ops, “you are a genius ninja” doesn’t fix a messy digest. A one-line incident-scribe role that optimizes for BLOCKER calls does. We’ll drop costume bloat and keep job, optimize-for, and refuse-list.

*(0:55–1:45)*  
You’ll separate **hard** constraints from **soft** preferences, add a **priority** line when they collide, and demand tables, JSON, and checklists that a teammate—or a script—can consume without cleanup. Then you’ll lock audience, tone, and brand voice without sounding like a parody of Jordan’s inbox.

*(1:45–2:35)*  
Expect about an hour: this intro, four lessons with interactives, a diagram of the Role · Hard · Soft · Format stack, and a live demo that turns a fuzzy rewrite into JSON plus a tone lock.

*(2:35–3:20)*  
Success looks like this: you can brief a model for Acme Ops Support or standup, fence what must never break, and get an answer that pastes cleanly into a doc, ticket, or parser. Let’s ship prompts that behave under pressure.

*(3:20–3:50)*  
Open Lesson 1 — Role prompting that actually helps — when you’re ready.
