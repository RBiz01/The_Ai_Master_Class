Rewrite a costume role

Turn theatrical persona fluff into a role that changes decisions.

Instructions

  1. Read the costume role below (Acme Ops).
  2. Rewrite it with a real job, an optimize-for target, and an optional refuse-list.
  3. Click Check role. Fix anything the checker flags.
  4. You're done when all success criteria turn green.

Costume role (rewrite this)

You are a world-class genius ninja rockstar ops wizard with 40 years of experience and unmatched brilliance. Help with the vendor mess.

Your role rewrite

Name a concrete job (incident scribe, vendor-risk analyst, support editor), say what to optimize for (BLOCKER calls, calm replies), and list refuses (no speculation, no blame, no fake vendors).

You're done when

  • Names a concrete job or stance (not “genius/ninja”)
  • States what to optimize for
  • Includes a refuse / must-not / avoid line
  • Avoids costume bloat words (ninja, rockstar, overlord, genius wizard)
Nice work. That role changes decisions. Capture one takeaway, then continue the module.
