# Module 4 example video — Decompose a messy launch checklist

**Length:** ~5–8 minutes  
**Scene:** Screen capture + talk-over  
**Cast:** Instructor (VO) · fictional Acme Ops Launch  
**Safety:** `learner@example.com`, kit `AO-1042`, Wave-2, no real PII

---

## Setup (show on screen)

**Fat ask (before)**

> “Figure out launch readiness for Acme Ops Wave-2, who owns each risk, and write the Monday all-hands blurb for learner@example.com.”

**Cliffs**

- Invented ship dates  
- Mixed exec + customer tone  
- Owners guessed without a risk table

---

## Talk-over + demo beats

### Beat 1 — Show the fat failure (0:00–1:00)
Paste the fat ask. Show a single response that invents “ships Friday,” mixes audiences, and buries owners in prose. VO: “One prompt, three jobs, zero done-when.”

### Beat 2 — Draw the pipeline (1:00–2:20)
On screen, sketch:

```text
1) Extract blockers/watch → markdown table
2) Propose owners → Owner | Action | Due
3) Draft all-hands blurb from approved table → ≤100 words
```

VO: “Pipeline. Output of each step feeds the next.”

### Beat 3 — Sub-task 1 live (2:20–3:30)
Run extract-only prompt. Highlight table. Strike any invented date.

### Beat 4 — Sub-tasks 2–3 (3:30–5:20)
Owners from the table. Then blurb with plan-then-answer: SCRATCHPAD 3 bullets, FINAL note only. VERIFY: no invented dates, ≤100 words, internal tone.

### Beat 5 — Close (5:20–6:30)
Side-by-side: fat ask vs three cards. CTA: “Order your own pipeline in Lesson 3’s interactive.”
