## Talk-over (narration)

*(0:00–0:25)*  
Welcome to Module 4 of **Prompt Engineering Mastery** on The Ai Master Class. Exemplars taught what good looks like. Now we decide when the model should *show its work*—and when a tidy chain is just ceremony.

*(0:25–1:05)*  
This hour you’ll learn when to ask for reasoning, how to run **plan-then-answer** so scratchpads never leak into the paste, how to **decompose** fat multi-step asks, and how to attach a short **VERIFY** checklist that catches invented ETAs and format drift.

*(1:05–1:55)*  
At fictional Acme Ops, a Wave-2 launch checklist is the poster child: inventory risks, assign owners, then draft the Monday blurb. One mega-prompt invents dates and mixes audiences. A pipeline ships clean.

*(1:55–2:40)*  
You’ll study a decompose-flow diagram, complete four interactives with success criteria, and watch a live demo splitting a messy launch ask into sub-tasks. About sixty minutes end to end.

*(2:40–3:20)*  
Success looks like this: reasoning on demand, FINAL that pastes without scrubbing, and self-checks that catch the cliffs you actually fall off—not a chain-of-thought sermon on every email.

*(3:20–3:50)*  
Open Lesson 1 — When to ask for reasoning — when you’re ready.
