# Module 4 — Chain-of-thought and decomposition

**Course:** Prompt Engineering Mastery  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Help learners decide when visible reasoning helps versus when it wastes time or leaks sloppy logic. Cover plan-then-answer patterns, breaking multi-step work into sub-prompts, and lightweight self-checks. Emphasize judgment: not every email needs a chain-of-thought sermon.

## Why this module ships fourth

Exemplars teach *what good looks like*. Chain-of-thought and decomposition teach *how to think through complexity*—and when to keep the scratchpad off the final deliverable.
