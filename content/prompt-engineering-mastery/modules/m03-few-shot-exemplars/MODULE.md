## Goal

Show when zero-shot is enough and when exemplars earn their tokens. Learners will write positive exemplars that teach structure and tone, add contrast/near-miss examples, and budget tokens so quality beats quantity. Goal: exemplars that generalize to next Monday’s paste—not copy-paste theater.

## Why this module ships third

Roles and formats tell the model *what shape* to produce. Exemplars show *what good looks like*—especially for tone, edge cases, and “almost right” traps Acme Ops hits every week.
