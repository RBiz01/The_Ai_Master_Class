## Talk-over (narration)

*(0:00–0:25)*  
Welcome to Module 3 of **Prompt Engineering Mastery** on The Ai Master Class. Clear instructions and formats get you far. When tone, policy boundaries, or “almost right” answers keep slipping, you teach by example.

*(0:25–1:00)*  
This hour you’ll decide when **zero-shot** is enough and when **few-shot** earns its tokens. You’ll write exemplars that teach the pattern—not trivia—add contrast and near-misses, and trim for token budget so quality beats a pile of clones.

*(1:00–1:50)*  
At fictional Acme Ops, a customer delay reply is a classic spend: same JSON keys every time, but the model needs to see credit-vs-apology and “never invent an ETA.” We’ll build two golds and one near-miss that prevent the expensive failure.

*(1:50–2:40)*  
You’ll study an exemplar anatomy diagram, complete four interactives with success criteria, and watch a live demo crafting the Acme Ops reply set. About sixty minutes end to end.

*(2:40–3:20)*  
Success looks like this: a lean exemplar pack another teammate can paste next Monday without babysitting the model. Let’s make few-shot a deliberate tool—not a habit of pasting more text.

*(3:20–3:50)*  
Open Lesson 1 — Zero-shot vs few-shot — when you’re ready.
