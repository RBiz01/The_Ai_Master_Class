## Setup (show on screen)

**Rubric**

1. Facts — no invented dates/ETAs  
2. Format — JSON only  
3. Policy — 10% credit only if slip confirmed

**Outputs A & B** — as in Lesson 1 interactive.

## Talk-over + demo beats

### Beat 1 — Vibes trap (0:00–0:50)
Read both aloud quickly. VO: “Both feel busy. Only one is shippable.”

### Beat 2 — Score A (0:50–2:20)
Mark Facts/Format/Policy/Tone pass. Highlight absence of dates and correct offer.

### Beat 3 — Score B (2:20–4:20)
Red marks: invented Friday, essay wrapper, full refund. Tone muddy. VO: “Automatic do-not-ship.”

### Beat 4 — Patch + golden re-run (4:20–5:40)
Add `Never invent dates not in CONTEXT` + `JSON only`. Re-check cliff case. Changelog: `v1.1 — ETA + format hard constraints`.

### Beat 5 — Close (5:40–6:30)
CTA: “Build your happy/edge/cliff set in Lesson 2.”
