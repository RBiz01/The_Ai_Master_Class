# Lesson 2.1 — Capture your voice with exemplars

**Module:** Writing that sounds like you  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Build a tiny voice pack from real Acme Ops writing samples.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Capture your voice with exemplars).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Build a tiny voice pack from real Acme Ops writing samples.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Exemplars teach voice

Paste 2–3 short samples *you* wrote. Label what to copy: length, warmth, directness.

**Acme Ops voice pack stub**

- Sample A: crisp Slack unblock ask  
- Sample B: calm customer delay email to `learner@example.com`  
- Sample C: exec risk bullet with one ask

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Don’t say ‘sound like me.’ Show three receipts.”

*(Beat 2)* “Exemplars beat adjectives. ‘Professional’ means nothing; your last email means everything.”

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Two or three real samples beat ten adjectives.
