# Lesson 2.4 — Editing passes: tighten, tone-check, brand-lock

**Module:** Writing that sounds like you  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Run a three-pass edit checklist before anything leaves your desk.

![Lesson video](assets/l04-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l04-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Three editing passes

1. **Tighten** — cut 20% words  
2. **Tone-check** — match audience (exec vs teammate vs customer)  
3. **Brand-lock** — Acme Ops terms, no invented product names

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Draft is free. Send is expensive. Pay the three-pass tax.”

## Practice

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Tighten → tone → brand. Then send.
