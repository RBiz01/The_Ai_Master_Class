# Lesson 3.3 — Multi-source synthesis briefs

**Module:** Research & synthesis without hallucinations  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Merge 2–3 sources into one brief; surface conflicts explicitly.

## Lesson video

![Lesson video](assets/l03-video.mp4)

**Video:** `assets/l03-video.mp4` — example/theme clip for this lesson.

## Teaching

### Synthesis with conflicts

When sources disagree, **don’t average**. Table the conflict and recommend a check.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “A brief that hides conflict is a liability. Surface it.”

## Practice

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Merge sources. Highlight conflicts. Assign a check.
