# Lesson 3.2 — Claim → Evidence → Confidence tags

**Module:** Research & synthesis without hallucinations  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

## Learning objective

Structure research answers as claim / evidence / confidence.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Claim → Evidence → Confidence tags).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Structure research answers as claim / evidence / confidence.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Claim · Evidence · Confidence

Force the shape:

| Claim | Evidence (quote) | Confidence |
|-------|------------------|------------|
| SSL install slips to Fri 2pm CT | “Earliest install Friday 2pm CT” | High |

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Opinions without quotes are cosplay research.”

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Every claim needs a quote and a confidence tag.
