**Module:** Research & synthesis without hallucinations

## Learning objective

Force the model to work only from pasted Acme Ops source text.

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.

3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Grounding: paste sources, don't invent).

## Teaching

### Paste the source or don’t ask for facts

```text
Use ONLY the text between <source> tags. If the answer isn’t there, say “Not in source.”
<source>…paste…</source>
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Models invent politely. Your job is to starve invention with sources.”

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

No source paste → no factual ask.
