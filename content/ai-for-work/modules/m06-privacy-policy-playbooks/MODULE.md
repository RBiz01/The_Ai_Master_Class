**Slug:** `m06-privacy-policy-playbooks`

## Goal

Know what not to paste, map vendor policies to company rules, publish team playbooks, and govern shared prompts with versioning and review.

## Why this module ships here

Speed without guardrails creates incidents. Module 6 makes safe defaults the easy path for Acme Ops.
