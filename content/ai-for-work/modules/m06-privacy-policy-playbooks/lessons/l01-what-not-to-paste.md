# Lesson 6.1 — What not to paste (data classes)

**Module:** Privacy, policy, and team playbooks  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Sort example snippets into public, internal, and restricted buckets.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: What not to paste (data classes)).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Sort example snippets into public, internal, and restricted buckets.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Data classes

| Class | Examples | Paste? |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Public | Marketing copy | OK |
| Internal | Portal go-live checklist | Careful / redact |
| Restricted | Customer PII, credentials, payroll | **Never** into consumer AI |

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Speed is not a reason to paste `learner@example.com` lists into a consumer chat.”

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Public / internal / restricted. Restricted never pastes.
