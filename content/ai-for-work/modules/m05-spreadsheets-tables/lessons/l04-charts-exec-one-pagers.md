# Lesson 5.4 — Charts and executive one-pagers

**Module:** Spreadsheets, tables, and light analysis

## Learning objective

Turn a table into an exec brief: headline, 3 bullets, one ask.

## What to do

1. Cue up the **Lesson video** and watch it once before you dig into the text.
2. Read **Teaching** carefully — especially the worked example you can reuse Monday.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Charts and executive one-pagers).
4. Apply the idea once in your words (sticky note or note app is fine).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Turn a table into an exec brief: headline, 3 bullets, one ask.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.
