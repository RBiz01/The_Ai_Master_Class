# Lesson 5.4 — Charts and executive one-pagers

**Module:** Spreadsheets, tables, and light analysis  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Turn a table into an exec brief: headline, 3 bullets, one ask.

![Lesson video](assets/l04-video.mp4)

## Teaching

### Exec one-pager

Headline (≤12 words) · 3 bullets · 1 ask · optional chart description (not decoration).

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Execs buy clarity. Charts support; they don’t substitute.”

## Practice

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Headline, three bullets, one ask.
