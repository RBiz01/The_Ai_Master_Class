# Lesson 5.3 — Light analysis: trends, outliers, asks

**Module:** Spreadsheets, tables, and light analysis  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l03-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Ask for trends and outliers with explicit caveats—no fake precision.

![Lesson video](assets/l03-video.mp4)

## Teaching

### Trends with caveats

Require: trend statement, 1–2 outliers, **caveat** (sample size / missing weeks), and one recommended ask.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “No fake precision. Acme Ops would rather be roughly right.”

Open `l03-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Trend · outlier · caveat · ask.
