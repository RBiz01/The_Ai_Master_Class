# Lesson 5.2 — Formulas and pivots explained in plain English

**Module:** Spreadsheets, tables, and light analysis  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Request formula/pivot explanations you could teach a teammate.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Formulas and pivots explained in plain English).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Request formula/pivot explanations you could teach a teammate.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.
