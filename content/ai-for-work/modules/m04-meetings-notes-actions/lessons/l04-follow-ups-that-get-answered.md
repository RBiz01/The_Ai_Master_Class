# Lesson 4.4 — Follow-ups that get answered

**Module:** Meetings, notes, and action plans  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l04-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Write crisp follow-ups with one ask and a reply deadline.

## What to do

1. Cue up the **Lesson video** and watch it once before you dig into the text.
2. Read **Teaching** carefully — especially the worked example you can reuse Monday.
3. Open `l04-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Follow-ups that get answered).
4. Apply the idea once in your words (sticky note or note app is fine).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Write crisp follow-ups with one ask and a reply deadline.* and `l04-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Follow-ups that get answers

Subject + 3 lines + **one ask** + reply-by date. CC only people who must act.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “A follow-up with three asks gets zero replies.”

## Practice

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

One ask. One deadline. Send.
