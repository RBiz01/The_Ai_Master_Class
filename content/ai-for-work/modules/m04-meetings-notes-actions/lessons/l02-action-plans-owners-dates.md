# Lesson 4.2 — Action plans with owners and dates

**Module:** Meetings, notes, and action plans  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l02-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Extract actions as Owner · Task · Due — never invent owners.

## What to do

1. Play the **Lesson video** all the way through — treat it as the cold open.
2. Read **Teaching** and the worked micro-example like a playbook, not a skim.
3. Open `l02-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: Action plans with owners and dates).
4. Capture a one-sentence Monday-morning takeaway you could paste into Slack.
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Extract actions as Owner · Task · Due — never invent owners.* and `l02-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Owner · Task · Due

```text
Extract actions as a table: Owner | Task | Due date.
If owner/date missing, write UNKNOWN — do not invent names or dates.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Invented owners create ghost work. Prefer UNKNOWN.”

## Practice

Open `l02-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Never invent owners or dates. Mark UNKNOWN.
