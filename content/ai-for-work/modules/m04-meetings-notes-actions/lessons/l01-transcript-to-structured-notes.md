# Lesson 4.1 — From transcript to structured notes

**Module:** Meetings, notes, and action plans  
**Duration:** ~6–8 min teaching + ~4 min interactive  
**Interactive:** `l01-interactive.html`

---

## Learning objective

Map raw transcript text into Decisions, Risks, and Open questions.

## What to do

1. Watch the **Lesson video** once for the visual hook.
2. Read **Teaching** (and the worked example) without rushing.
3. Open `l01-interactive.html` and follow its Instructions until you hit the success state (this locks in: From transcript to structured notes).
4. Rewrite or apply the idea once in your own words (one sentence is enough).
5. **You’re done when:** you can explain — in plain language — *Map raw transcript text into Decisions, Risks, and Open questions.* and `l01-interactive.html` shows success.

## Teaching

### Transcript → structure

Ask for three buckets only: **Decisions**, **Risks**, **Open questions**. Ignore banter.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* “Notes are for operators, not novelists.”

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Decisions · Risks · Open questions. That’s the skeleton.
