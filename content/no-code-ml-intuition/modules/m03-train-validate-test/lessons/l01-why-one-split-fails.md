## Teaching

### The take-home exam problem

Studying the answer key then taking the same test measures memory, not learning. Training and scoring on the **same rows** does the same thing.

### What honest means

Hold out rows the training process never sees. Score only there.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* One pile used twice is a vanity metric.
*(Beat 2)* Split first. Train second. Brag third.
*(Beat 3)* No holdout score means no result yet.
