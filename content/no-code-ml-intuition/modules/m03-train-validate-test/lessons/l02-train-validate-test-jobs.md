## Teaching

### Three jobs, three piles

| Split | Job | Must not |
|-------|-----|----------|
| Train | Fit knobs | Report final success alone |
| Validate | Tune choices | Pretend it is final proof |
| Test | Final exam | Keep peeking and re-tuning |

### Acme Ops rule

Validate while exploring. Touch test **once** before ship.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Train fits. Validate tunes. Test tells the truth once.
*(Beat 2)* Reusing test burns honesty.
*(Beat 3)* Write split sizes on the experiment card.
