## Teaching

### Two error types

- **False positive** — cried wolf (hurts precision)
- **False negative** — missed the real issue (hurts recall)

### Northstar costs

- FP on hot lead: wasted AE time
- FN on hot lead: missed revenue

Pick the pain you can afford; set thresholds accordingly.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Precision: of our alarms, how many were real?
*(Beat 2)* Recall: of the real cases, how many did we catch?
*(Beat 3)* Dollars choose the tradeoff.
