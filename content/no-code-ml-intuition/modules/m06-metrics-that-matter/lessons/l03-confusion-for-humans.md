## Teaching

### The 2x2 you can draw on a whiteboard

Predicted yes/no vs actual yes/no yields TP, FN, FP, TN.

Practice: We caught TP, missed FN, falsely alarmed FP.

### Founder translation

Do not say F1. Say: We miss about 2 real escalations a week and raise 5 false alarms.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Confusion matrices are four buckets.
*(Beat 2)* Translate buckets into weekly counts.
*(Beat 3)* If a founder cannot repeat it, rewrite it.
