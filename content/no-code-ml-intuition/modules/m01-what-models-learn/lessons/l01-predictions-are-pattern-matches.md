## Teaching

### The Monday-morning myth

At **Northstar Studio**, a producer asks whether the model can understand which briefs will convert.

No. It can **match patterns** from past briefs that looked similar to winners. That is powerful — and different from understanding.

### Pattern match vs comprehension

| What people say | What is actually happening |
|-----------------|----------------------------|
| The model knows our brand | It saw enough labeled examples to imitate past outcomes |
| It reasoned about the client | It weighted features correlated with the label |
| It will be right next quarter | Only if the world stays similar to training |

### Three durable sentences

1. **Training** = show examples with answers (labels).
2. **Prediction** = guess the answer for a new example.
3. **Error** = how often those guesses miss — on data the model did not train on.

### Talk-over narration (instructor VO)

*(Beat 1)* Models do not understand — they match patterns from examples.
*(Beat 2)* Northstar's brief scorer is not psychic; it studied last year's winners.
*(Beat 3)* Translate 'the AI knows' into 'the AI matched.'
