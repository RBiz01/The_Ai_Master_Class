## Teaching

### Missing is not neutral

Imputing averages or adding 'unknown' can hide patterns (missing budget often means tire-kickers).

### Messy categories

Meta / meta ads / Facebook / FB look like four channels until you normalize.

### Checklist

- Quantify % missing
- Drop, impute, or flag
- Normalize categories
- Document in the experiment log

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Nulls are process data.
*(Beat 2)* Category mess splits signal.
*(Beat 3)* Clean once; models get smarter.
