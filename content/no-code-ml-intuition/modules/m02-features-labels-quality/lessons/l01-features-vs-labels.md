## Teaching

### The two columns that matter

- **Features** = what you know at prediction time.
- **Label** = the answer you predict.

### Northstar lead table

| Feature examples | Label |
|------------------|-------|
| Industry, budget band, channel | Booked discovery call? |

Columns only known after the outcome are spoilers, not features.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Features are available at score time; labels are answers.
*(Beat 2)* Write the prediction moment on a sticky.
*(Beat 3)* Mixing them makes demos shine and production fail.
