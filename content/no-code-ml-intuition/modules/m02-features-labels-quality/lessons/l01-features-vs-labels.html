Teaching

The two columns that matter

  • Features = what you know at prediction time.
  • Label = the answer you predict.

Feature examplesLabel

Columns only known after the outcome are spoilers, not features.

(Beat 1) Features are available at score time; labels are answers. (Beat 2) Write the prediction moment on a sticky. (Beat 3) Mixing them makes demos shine and production fail.
