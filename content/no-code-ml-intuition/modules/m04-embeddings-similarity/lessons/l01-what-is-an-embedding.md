## Teaching

### Coordinates for meaning

An **embedding** is a list of numbers that places an item where nearby means similar meaning.

### What embeddings are not

- Not full understanding
- Not a guarantee of truth
- Not a replacement for access control or freshness

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* Embeddings turn text into coordinates for similarity.
*(Beat 2)* Close in space approximates close in meaning.
*(Beat 3)* You retrieve neighbors, not oracles.
