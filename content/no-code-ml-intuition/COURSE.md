## Who this is for

Operators, marketers, founders, and creators who want machine learning intuition without drowning in calculus—especially people shipping with no-code AutoML, classifiers, and embedding search.

No coding required. Concepts transfer across major no-code ML and AI search tools.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Explain what models **actually learn** (pattern matching from examples).
2. Separate **features** from **labels** and hunt leakage.
3. Use **train / validate / test** without fooling yourself.
4. Apply **embeddings & similarity search** for retrieval.
5. Design a **one-week no-code experiment** with kill criteria.
6. Read **metrics that matter** to the business—not vanity accuracy.

## Prerequisites

- Curiosity about ML outcomes (no math degree)
- Access to any no-code ML or embedding-search tool (optional for practice)
- A fictional or sandbox workflow (**Northstar Studio**, **Acme Ops**)

### Module 1 — What models actually learn *(built)*

1. Predictions are pattern matches, not magic  
2. Parameters, weights, and fitting the curve  
3. Underfitting vs overfitting in plain English  
4. What models cannot learn from your data  

### Module 2 — Features, labels, and data quality *(built)*

1. Features vs labels without the jargon  
2. Signal, noise, and leakage  
3. Missing data and messy columns  
4. Label quality beats fancy algorithms  

### Module 3 — Train / validate / test without the jargon trap *(built)*

1. Why one split isn't enough  
2. Train / validate / test jobs  
3. Cross-validation intuition (no math)  
4. Avoiding the look-ahead trap  

### Module 4 — Embeddings & similarity search *(built)*

1. What an embedding actually is  
2. Similarity search for creators & ops  
3. Chunking and retrieval that works  
4. When embeddings beat keywords (and when not)  

### Module 5 — No-code experiments you can ship *(built)*

1. Pick a question before you pick a tool  
2. No-code ML platforms: a field guide  
3. Design a one-week experiment  
4. Ship, measure, kill or keep  

### Module 6 — Reading metrics that matter to the business *(built)*

1. Accuracy is often the wrong scoreboard  
2. Precision, recall, and business cost  
3. Confusion matrices for humans  
4. Dashboards execs will actually read
