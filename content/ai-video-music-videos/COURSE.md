## Who this is for

Creators, marketers, course builders, and creative operators who need **shippable motion**: text-to-video clips, presenter/narration lessons, and locked-look music-video assembly—without gambling on vibe prompts.

Comfortable with chat UIs and basic editors helps. No cinema camera required. Model-agnostic patterns (Grok Imagine as one T2V path; HeyGen/Hyperframes as one presenter path).

**Differentiator:** This is **MOTION + MUSIC VIDEO assembly**—not the `generative-media` survey, not `ai-music-production` (audio-first), not `ai-image-studio` (stills-first).

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. Write **video briefs** with aspect, duration, look lock, and shot lists that beat vague vibe prompts.
2. Generate **text-to-video** one-shots with named camera moves and four-clip continuity.
3. Produce **presenter & narration** clips (avatar or VO) using the course/lesson video pattern.
4. Run a **music-video pipeline**: style lock, beat-grid cuts in 16:9, stitch without look drift.
5. **Edit & ship** selects with titles, platform exports, and rights/likeness hygiene.
6. Install a **Video OS**—shot library, brief templates, handoffs from music/stills—and ship one 60-second piece.

## Prerequisites

- Access to at least one T2V tool and one presenter or VO path
- A simple timeline editor (any NLE or web stitcher)
- Optional: handoff assets from music or stills workflows (habits transfer)
