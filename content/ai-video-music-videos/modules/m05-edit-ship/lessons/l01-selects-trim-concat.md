## Teaching

### Selects → trim → concat

Edit order at **Northstar Studio**:

1. **Selects reel** — only scorecard passes
2. **Trim** — remove morph tails, hold frames, audio pops
3. **Concat** — join on beat markers; check frame blends

```text
SELECTS FOLDER
/project/aurora/selects/A_pass/
Rejects stay in /rejects for learning—never in the timeline
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Selects are a quality gate, not a junk drawer."

*(Beat 2)* "Trim before concat. Concat before titles."

### Worked micro-example

Twelve generates → five selects → four trims → one concat sequence on the beat grid.
