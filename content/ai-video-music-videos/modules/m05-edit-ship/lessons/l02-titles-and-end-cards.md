## Teaching

### Titles and end cards

Titles must honor the look lock and platform safe areas.

- Opening title ≤ 3s on first downbeats
- Lower-thirds ≤ 7 words
- End card: title, **Northstar Studio**, CTA, `learner@example.com` only as fictional contact
- No real-person faces on cards

Palette: dark `#0b0f19`, accents `#7c5cff` / `#ec4899`.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Titles are part of the look—not a default white Arial sticker."

*(Beat 2)* "End cards close the loop: who, what next, where."

### Worked micro-example

End card: "Aurora Pulse · Northstar Studio · Watch the cut" on brand gradient.
