## Teaching

### Rights and likeness

Ship gate for **Northstar Studio**:

- Track license cleared (or fictional training track labeled as such)
- No real-person likeness; no NSFW
- AI disclosure per platform/policy when required
- Talent/avatar license if using a vendor presenter path
- Internal contact stub: `learner@example.com` — never real personal emails

```text
RIGHTS CHECKLIST
[ ] Music rights
[ ] Likeness / avatar terms
[ ] Disclosure note drafted
[ ] No scraped celebrity faces
[ ] Client-safe stills sources
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "A viral cut with a rights bomb is not a win."

*(Beat 2)* "Likeness policy is part of the brief—not a post-publish scramble."
