# Module 5 — Edit & ship

**Slug:** `m05-edit-ship`  
**Course:** AI Video & Music Videos  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

## Goal

Select, trim, and concat clips; add titles and end cards; export for platforms; and clear rights and likeness before publish.

## Why this module ships now

Generate without ship is shelfware. Module 5 turns selects into platform-ready masters with rights hygiene.
