**Status:** Fully built

## Goal

Choose avatar vs VO-only, write scripts for the ear, layer Hyperframes-style graphics with presenters, and ship the course/lesson video pattern used across The Ai Master Class.

## Why this module ships now

Music videos need motion; course and promo clips need a human voice. Module 3 covers presenter paths (HeyGen/Hyperframes as one option) without locking you to one vendor click-path.
