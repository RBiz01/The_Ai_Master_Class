## Teaching

### Write for listening

Eye scripts fail in the ear.

**Eye:** "Leveraging synergistic multimodal pipelines…"
**Ear:** "Here's the move. Lock the look. Cut to the beat. Ship the sixty."

Rules:

- Short sentences
- One idea per beat
- Speak numbers and CTAs clearly
- Mark pauses `(beat)` for avatar timing

```text
EAR SCRIPT — 45s lesson open
(0:00) Hook — one concrete pain
(0:08) Promise — what they'll can do
(0:20) Three beats — teach
(0:38) Practice callout
(0:42) Takeaway line
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "If you stumble reading it aloud, the avatar will too."

*(Beat 2)* "Write like you talk at Northstar Studio stand-up—not like a white paper."

### Worked micro-example

Rewrite a jargon paragraph into five spoken lines with timestamps.
