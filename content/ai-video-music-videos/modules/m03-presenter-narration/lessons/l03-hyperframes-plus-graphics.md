## Teaching

### Presenter + graphics

Hyperframes-style (or similar) presenter + graphics pattern:

1. Avatar / VO plate
2. Lower-third title (safe margins)
3. Full-screen diagram for 4–6s
4. Return to presenter for takeaway
5. End card CTA

Keep graphics in the **look lock palette** (`#7c5cff` / `#ec4899` on `#0b0f19` for this course brand).

```text
GRAPHICS STACK
- Never cover the mouth/eyes on avatar close-ups
- Max 7 words on lower-thirds
- Diagrams: one idea per card
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Graphics teach. Presenters guide. Don't make them fight."

*(Beat 2)* "Hyperframes is one assembly path—same stack works elsewhere."

### Worked micro-example

Lesson on look lock: avatar open → SVG diagram full screen → avatar close with takeaway.
