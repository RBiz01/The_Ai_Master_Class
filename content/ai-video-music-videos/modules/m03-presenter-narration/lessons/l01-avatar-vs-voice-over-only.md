## Teaching

### Avatar vs VO-only

Presenter path choices at **Northstar Studio**:

| Path | Use when | Watch-outs |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Avatar presenter** (HeyGen-style is one path) | Trust, course lessons, talking-head hooks | Likeness policy, uncanny frames |
| **Voice-over only** | Music-forward cuts, B-roll led stories | Needs strong visuals; no face cue |
| **Hybrid** | Lesson pattern: VO + occasional avatar open/close | Keep look lock across both |

No real-person likeness. Fictional or licensed avatars only. Contact: `learner@example.com` for internal review stubs.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Face on camera is a product choice, not a default."

*(Beat 2)* "HeyGen or Hyperframes can be the presenter path—patterns transfer if you change tools."

### Worked micro-example

Course lesson → avatar + graphics. Music-video chorus → VO-none, track-led B-roll.
