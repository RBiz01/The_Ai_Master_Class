## Teaching

### The usable-clip bar

At **Northstar Studio**, a clip is not "cool AI output." It is usable when it clears four gates:

1. **Subject** — who/what is on screen is readable in the first second
2. **Motion** — camera or subject motion is intentional, not random morph
3. **Audio plan** — silence, bed, or VO is decided *before* generate
4. **Export intent** — 16:9 master, 9:16 cutdown, or both

```text
Usable clip checklist for <project>:
- Subject (one sentence):
- Motion (named move or static):
- Audio (silent / track / VO):
- Aspect + duration:
- Pass/fail notes:
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Pretty is not usable. Usable survives the stitch."

*(Beat 2)* "If you can't name the subject and the motion, regenerate—don't hope the timeline will fix it."

### Worked micro-example

**Vague:** "Neon city vibe, cinematic."
**Usable:** "Wide night street, single cyclist left→right, slow dolly forward, silent bed, 16:9, 4s, teal/magenta look lock."
