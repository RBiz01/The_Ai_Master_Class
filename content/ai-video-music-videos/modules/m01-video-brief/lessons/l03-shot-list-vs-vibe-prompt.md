## Teaching

### Shot list beats vibe

Vibe prompts feel creative. They waste tokens.

**Vibe:** "Epic dreamy night energy, vibes."
**Shot list (Northstar Studio):**

1. Wide establishing — empty street, neon puddles, static, 4s
2. Medium — courier enters frame left, slow pan right, 4s
3. Close — helmet reflection of signage, micro push-in, 3s
4. Wide exit — courier exits right, hold empty frame 1s, 4s

```text
Shot list format:
# | Size | Action | Camera | Duration | Look-lock ref
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "If you can't number the shots, you don't have a plan."

*(Beat 2)* "Vibe is for the look lock paragraph. Shots are for the timeline."

### Worked micro-example

Feed the numbered list to your T2V tool (Grok Imagine is one path). Generate one clip per row. Continuity lives in the look-lock ref column.
