## Teaching

### Aspect, duration, look lock

Three decisions that save regenerations at **Northstar Studio**:

| Lock | Example | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| Aspect | 16:9 master | Music-video stitch + YouTube |
| Duration | 4s / 8s clips | Beat-grid friendly |
| Look lock | 1 paragraph | Palette, lens, grain, subject treatment |

```text
LOOK LOCK — Northstar Studio / Project Aurora
Palette: deep teal #0b3d4a, magenta #ec4899 accents, soft film grain
Lens: 35mm equivalent, shallow DOF on hero, soft bokeh
Subject: stylized courier (no real-person likeness)
Lighting: dusk neon bounce, no midday sun
Do-not: cartoon rubber, oversharpen, random wardrobe changes
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Aspect and duration are product decisions, not afterthoughts."

*(Beat 2)* "A look lock is a contract. Every clip either honors it or gets cut."

### Worked micro-example

**Bad:** "Make it cinematic and pretty."
**Good:** Paste the LOOK LOCK block above into every T2V prompt as immutable context.
