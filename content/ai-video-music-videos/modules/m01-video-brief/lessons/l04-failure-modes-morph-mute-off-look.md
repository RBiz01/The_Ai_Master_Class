## Teaching

### Morph, mute, off-look

Three failure modes that destroy a music-video stitch:

| Failure | Symptom | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| **Morph** | Faces/limbs melt; objects invent themselves | Regenerate; tighten subject + do-nots |
| **Mute** | Expected VO/bed missing or clipped | Check audio plan; re-export or re-gen with audio path |
| **Off-look** | Palette/lens/wardrobe drift vs look lock | Cut from selects; do not "grade it into" the set |

```text
Score each take 0–2 on: morph risk · audio OK · look lock match
≤3 total → regenerate. 4–5 → salvage only if stitch-critical. 6 → keep.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Morph is not a vibe. It's a reject."

*(Beat 2)* "Off-look clips poison the whole cut. Be ruthless in selects."

### Worked micro-example

Courier clip #3 goes magenta→orange and helmet shape drifts. Score: morph 1, audio 2, look 0 = 3 → regenerate with stronger look-lock paste.
