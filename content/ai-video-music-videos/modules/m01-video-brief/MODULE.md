# Module 1 — Video brief

**Slug:** `m01-video-brief`  
**Course:** AI Video & Music Videos  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Write briefs that produce usable motion clips: aspect, duration, look lock, shot lists that beat vague vibe prompts, and failure modes you catch before stitch.

## Why this module ships first

Most AI video waste starts at the brief. Module 1 installs a brief OS so Northstar Studio stops regenerating for the wrong ratio, look, or mute audio.
