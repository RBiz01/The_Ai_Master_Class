## Teaching

### Build a shot library

After a few projects, **Northstar Studio** stops generating from zero.

Tag clips:

- Project · style card ID · shot size · camera · duration · score · rights OK

```text
LIBRARY QUERY
style:aurora_dusk AND size:wide AND score:>=8 AND rights:ok
```

Reusable B-roll beats music-video speed without look drift—if tags are honest.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Your future self is a teammate. Tag for them."

*(Beat 2)* "A library of rejects teaches failure modes. A library of selects ships faster."

### Worked micro-example

Next track in same style card pulls four wide dusk clips instead of regenerating establishing shots.

## Practice

Open `l01-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Tag selects honestly. Reuse under the same style card.
