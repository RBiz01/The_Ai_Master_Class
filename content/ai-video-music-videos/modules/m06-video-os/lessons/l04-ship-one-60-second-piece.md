## Teaching

### Ship a 60-second piece

Capstone for **Northstar Studio**:

Deliver one **~60-second** locked-look piece:

1. Brief + style card
2. Shot list (≈12–16 clips or fewer with holds)
3. T2V generates + selects
4. Beat-grid stitch (track-led or hybrid)
5. Titles + end card
6. Rights checklist
7. Export master 16:9 (+ optional 9:16)

Success = shipped file + notes, not infinite regenerations.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Done beats perfect. Sixty seconds. One look. Shipped."

*(Beat 2)* "This is your Video OS proof—brief to master."

### Worked micro-example

Aurora Pulse 60s cut: style locked, eight selects on grid, end card, rights checked, master exported.
