## Teaching

### Brief templates

Three reusable templates:

1. **T2V one-shot brief**
2. **Presenter / lesson brief**
3. **Music-video sequence brief**

Each includes: goal, look lock, shot list or script, audio role, rights notes, export intent.

```text
TEMPLATE — MV SEQUENCE
Track:
Style card ID:
Shot list rows:
Audio role: track-led
Export: 16:9 master + 9:16
Rights: …
Owner: learner@example.com (fictional)
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Templates make quality repeatable."

*(Beat 2)* "Monday morning should be fill-in-the-blanks—not reinvent the OS."

### Worked micro-example

Duplicate MV SEQUENCE template for "Aurora Pulse (radio edit)"—same style card, shorter beat map.
