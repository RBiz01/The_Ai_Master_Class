## Teaching

### Handoff from music & stills

This course is **motion + music-video assembly**. It accepts handoffs:

| From | Receives | Watch-outs |
|------|----------|------------|
| `ai-music-production`-style | Mixed track, BPM, sections | Don't re-gen music in this OS |
| `ai-image-studio`-style | Style refs, still keyframes | Stills ≠ motion continuity automatically |
| `generative-media` survey | Broad ideas | Narrow into brief + style card here |

```text
HANDOFF INTAKE
Track file + BPM + section map
Style refs (stills) → convert to look lock paragraph
Forbidden: inventing new musical arrangement in the video OS
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Stills inspire. Tracks time. Motion stitches."

*(Beat 2)* "Don't rebuild sibling courses—interface with them."

### Worked micro-example

Intake: still styleboard + 120 BPM mix → style card + beat map → T2V shot list.
