# Module 4 — Music video pipeline

**Slug:** `m04-music-video`  
**Course:** AI Video & Music Videos  
**Lessons:** 4 · **Est. time:** ~60 minutes  
**Status:** Fully built

---

## Goal

Run a locked-look music-video pipeline: one visual style, cut to the beat in 16:9, stitch without look drift, and choose track vs narration audio roles.

## Why this module ships now

This is the differentiator from generative-media surveys and from stills/music-only courses—motion assembled to music with continuity.
