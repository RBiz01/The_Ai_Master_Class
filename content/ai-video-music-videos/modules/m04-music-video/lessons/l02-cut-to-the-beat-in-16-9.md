## Teaching

### Beat grid in 16:9

Work the **16:9 master** first. Vertical cutdowns come later.

1. Mark downbeats on the track (fictional "Aurora Pulse")
2. Assign clip durations to beat intervals (e.g., 4 beats ≈ clip length)
3. Prefer cuts on downbeats; avoid mid-word if narration exists
4. Build on a grid, then micro-trim

```text
BEAT MAP (example @ 120 BPM)
0:00 downbeat — title
0:02 — clip A
0:06 — clip B
0:10 — clip C
…
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "If the cut doesn't hit the beat, viewers feel it before they can say it."

*(Beat 2)* "16:9 master first. 9:16 is a derivative, not the brain."

### Worked micro-example

Map eight 4-beat clips across the first 32 beats; adjust only after the grid feels musical.
