## Teaching

### Track vs narration

Audio roles:

| Role | When | Tips |
|------|------|------|
| **Track-led** | Music video body | Duck nothing; cuts follow beat |
| **Narration-led** | Lesson / promo explainers | Duck track −12 to −18 LU under VO |
| **Hybrid** | Cold open VO then track | Hard handoff on a downbeat |

Never fight the vocal with busy midrange SFX.

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Pick a leader: track or narration. Co-leaders muddy the mix."

*(Beat 2)* "Music videos sell the track. Course clips sell the teach."

### Worked micro-example

Aurora Pulse MV = track-led. Course trailer = 8s VO then track bed under end card.

## Takeaway

One audio leader. Duck the other. Hand off on a beat.
