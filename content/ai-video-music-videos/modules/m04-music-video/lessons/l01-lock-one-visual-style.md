## Teaching

### One style for the song

Music-video rule at **Northstar Studio**: **one visual style card per track**.

Freeze before any generate:

- Palette + references (still frames OK from image studio handoff)
- Subject treatment
- Motion vocabulary allowed
- Forbidden looks

Differentiate: this is **motion + music assembly**, not a generative-media survey and not stills-only or stems-only work.

```text
STYLE CARD — Track "Aurora Pulse" (fictional)
Look: teal/magenta dusk neon, soft grain
Motion: slow push + pan only
Forbid: daylight, cartoon, rapid whip pans
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "One song, one look. Variety comes from shots—not from new styles."

*(Beat 2)* "Lock style before you fall in love with a random beautiful outlier."

### Worked micro-example

Reject a gorgeous daylight clip because the style card says dusk neon only.
