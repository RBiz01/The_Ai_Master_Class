## Teaching

### Stitch without drift

Stitch selects only if they share the style card.

Drift killers:

- Mid-song palette shifts
- Grain on/off flicker
- Subject identity breaks
- Accidental daylight inserts

```text
STITCH GATE
Pass if: style card match + beat-aligned + no morph
Fail if: any off-look or morph in the candidate
Fix: regenerate that row — do not "grade away" identity drift
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "A beautiful off-look clip is still a fail."

*(Beat 2)* "Stitch is curation under a contract—not a collage of favorites."

### Worked micro-example

Timeline draft includes one orange-shifted take. Remove it; regenerate with style card pasted.
