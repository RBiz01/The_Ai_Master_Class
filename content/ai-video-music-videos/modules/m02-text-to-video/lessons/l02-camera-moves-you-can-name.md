## Teaching

### Name the camera move

Name moves models can approximate:

- **Static** — locked tripod feel
- **Slow push-in / pull-out** — gentle dolly
- **Pan left/right** — horizontal reveal
- **Orbit** — arc around subject (keep radius modest)
- **Handheld micro** — slight sway (use sparingly)

Avoid: "epic camera," "crazy cinematic move," impossible crane+orbit+whip in 3 seconds.

```text
Camera line template:
Camera: [move], [speed], [framing], [horizon locked|dutch OK]
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "If you can't name the move, the model will invent one."

*(Beat 2)* "One move per clip. Save complexity for the edit."

### Worked micro-example

**Bad:** "Insane cinematic camera energy."
**Good:** "Camera: slow push-in, medium framing, horizon locked, 4s."
