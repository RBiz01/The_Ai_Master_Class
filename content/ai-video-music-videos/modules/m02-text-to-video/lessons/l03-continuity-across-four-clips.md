## Teaching

### Four-clip continuity

Music-video assembly needs a **four-clip set** that could sit in one sequence:

Continuity checklist across clips A–D:

- Same subject identity (costume, silhouette)
- Same palette + grain (look lock)
- Compatible lighting time-of-day
- Matching lens language
- No sudden prop invents

```text
CONTINUITY CARD — Project Aurora clips A–D
Subject ID: stylized courier, matte black helmet, teal jacket
Palette: locked
Lighting: dusk neon only
Props: backpack only — no new props mid-set
Reject if: wardrobe drift, daylight bleed, cartoon rubber
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Continuity is a look lock applied four times—not four vibes."

*(Beat 2)* "Generate as a set. Review as a set. Reject as a set."

### Worked micro-example

Clips A,B,D match. Clip C invents a red scarf. Cut C; regenerate with "no scarf, teal jacket only."
