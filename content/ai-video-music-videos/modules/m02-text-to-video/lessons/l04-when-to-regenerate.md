## Teaching

### Regenerate with a scorecard

Stop polishing broken takes.

**Regen scorecard (0–2 each):** subject match · morph risk (invert) · look lock · usable duration · audio plan met

- **≤6** → regenerate with tighter prompt
- **7–8** → salvage trim OK
- **9–10** → selects reel

```text
Do not regenerate forever. Cap 3 attempts per shot list row, then revise the brief.
```

### Talk-over narration

*(Beat 1)* "Regenerate is a decision, not a vibe."

*(Beat 2)* "If attempt three still morphs, the brief—or the shot—is wrong."

### Worked micro-example

Shot 3 attempts: 4, 5, 5 → revise shot (simpler action) instead of attempt 4.

Open `l04-interactive.html` and rewrite the vague prompt until the checker lights green.

## Takeaway

Score takes. Cap attempts. Fix the brief when stuck.
